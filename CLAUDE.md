Layout (`src/financial_app/`, see ADR-0001):
- `domain/`: pure-Python calculations. It must never import NiceGUI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic or pywebview; `tests/test_architecture.py` enforces this. `domain/formatting.py` is the single Polish number and date formatter (`format_pln`, `format_percent`, `format_quantity`, `format_unit_price`, `format_date`, `format_datetime`, `format_count` (Polish plurals), and `format_exact` for edit fields) and parser of typed input (`parse_number`, `parse_date`); use it for every amount or date shown in or read from the UI.
- `persistence/`: SQLite through SQLAlchemy. `models.py` holds the ORM models, `migrations/` holds Alembic revisions, and `db.init_db(path)` applies them at start-up, right after `backup.backup_database` copies the file into `paths.backups_dir()` (newest 30 kept). The database lives in the user's app-data folder (`paths.data_dir()`, overridable with `FINANCIAL_APP_DATA_DIR`), never in the repo. Change the schema only through a new migration: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m alembic revision --autogenerate -m "..."`. `tests/test_database.py` fails if the models and migrations drift apart.
- `domain/accounts.py`: Account Types, the Tax Regime registry (`tax_regime_for`; OKI is a placeholder) and `AccountDraft`, which validates Account fields and raises `AccountError` with a Polish message. `persistence/accounts.py` saves Accounts (`list_accounts`, `add_account`, `update_account`) and enforces case-insensitive unique names.
- `domain/spreadsheet_import.py`: reads the Transakcje sheet's columns, classifies tickers (`EXCH:SYM`, bonds `TYP-DDMMRR` → Bond Series, `Gotówka`, `Waluty_<CUR>`), builds the Transaction for each row (Actual Amount = the sheet's "Total PLN") and fingerprints rows so a re-import skips them.
- `sources/spreadsheet.py`: opens the workbook (openpyxl), `preview_import` (matches Konta and Instruments, the user's `Decisions` resolve the rest, nothing saved) and `save_import` (creates the new Instruments and saves every row or none). `ui/import_sheet.py` is its section in Ustawienia. Transactions keep their Origin (`domain/transactions.py` `Origin`, migration 0011 adds `origin` and `external_id`).
- `domain/transactions.py`: Transaction Types (Deposit, Withdrawal, Buy, Sell, Dividend, Cost, Currency Exchange, DRIP, Split, Cash Transfer, Security Transfer so far), `TransactionDraft` validation, `buy_or_sell(...)` (builds a Buy/Sell and derives its Actual Amount; a foreign-currency one carries the user's optional `fx_rate` and the `NbpRate`, from which the `tax_amount` property follows, or a `BrokerConversion` (the PLN the broker charged and the Account's FX Conversion Fee %): the trade stores the percent, and its `fx_conversion_fee` and `effective_fx_rate` follow, the fee counting as a cost in the Tax Amount; `cash_currency` says whether it is paid from PLN or from foreign cash of the Instrument's currency, the commission always in PLN), `currency_exchange(...)` (PLN to a foreign Cash Currency or back, `to_pln`), `dividend(...)` (gross and withholding tax in the Instrument's or the chosen currency, the net amount into PLN at the user's rate or the NBP Rate, or the PLN the broker credited with a `BrokerConversion` (its fee a cost), or into foreign cash of that currency; no Instrument means interest), `cost(...)` (PLN, or from foreign cash valued at the NBP Rate), `drip(...)` (a Dividend reinvested into `quantity` units: a Lot costing the net amount, at the NBP Rate if foreign, no cash moving), `split(...)` (a `SplitRatio` X:Y, no amount), `cash_transfer(...)` (PLN to `target_account_id`, which `cash_balances` credits) and `security_transfer(...)` (units to `target_account_id`, no amount), `costs_by_account` (Costs, commissions and FX Conversion Fees) and `dividends_by_account`, `covering_exchange(buy)` (the "wymień walutę automatycznie" Exchange buying exactly the Buy's foreign amount), `cash_balances` (per Account, in any currency) and `lowest_cash_balance` (drives the insufficient-cash warning, per currency). `persistence/transactions.py` saves them (`add_transaction`, `add_transactions` (several at once, all or none), `update_transaction` and `delete_transaction`, each replaying the touched Positions to block a change that would leave a Sell or Security Transfer uncovered or a Split before the first Buy; `list_transactions`, newest first).
- `domain/lots.py`: the single FIFO engine (ADR-0003). `open_positions(transactions)` takes Transactions in entry order, sorts by date and the same-day order, and returns `Position`s (open `Lot`s with Actual `cost` and `tax_cost`, average price, value and result at a PLN price); a Split rescales open Lots (units × X/Y, costs kept) and a Security Transfer moves the oldest Lots to the target Account with their dates and costs; an uncovered Sell or Security Transfer raises `InsufficientQuantityError`, a Split before the Account held the Instrument `SplitBeforeFirstBuyError`. Same-day order: Split, Buy, Security Transfer, Sell, DRIP. `foreign_cash(transactions)` holds foreign cash as Lots per Account and currency (`ForeignCash`, with the realised FX result; spending more than held only warns and leaves a negative Lot), and `fx_result` sums an Account's realised and unrealised FX result.
- `domain/valuation.py`: `instrument_price(instrument, quote, rate_for)` decides the price a Position is valued at and its `PriceStatus`: the Manual Price while it is set (carrying the newest Quote to show beside it), else the newest Quote, else no price; no NBP Rate to convert it is its own status, and the rate is asked for only when there is a price. Its `Badge` (ręczna, nieaktualna when `stale`, bez źródła, do etapu 3 for `prices.STAGE_3_CLASSES`) shows beside the Position. The Portfolio only displays it.
- `domain/prices.py`: `Quote`, `PriceSourceError` (Polish message), the Price Source keys (`YAHOO`, `BOSSA`, ...) and `SOURCE_NAMES`, and `sources_to_try(asset_class_name, source_symbols)`: the Asset Class's Fallback Order (spec 4.1 defaults by class name; a class the user added follows "Inne"; editable per class with #41), keeping only the sources the Instrument has a Source Symbol for. Instruments carry `source_symbols` (source key to symbol) in `InstrumentDraft`, saved in `source_symbols` (migration 0012).
- `sources/yahoo.py`: `YahooSource(fetch).quote(symbol, currency)`, from the chart API: the price in the Instrument's quote currency (GBp ÷ 100; crypto in another currency through Yahoo's own `XXXYYY=X` rate; any other mismatch is a `PriceSourceError`), on the exchange's day (`regularMarketTime` + `gmtoffset`). Tests run on `tests/fixtures/yahoo/`.
- `sources/bossa.py`: `BossaSource(fetch).quote(symbol, "PLN")` from the Bossa JSON API (GPW shares, NewConnect, ETF and Catalyst bonds, searched in that order): one request per category per refresh (`refresh_prices` calls each source's `new_refresh` first, and a failed category is not asked again in that refresh); a listing without a positive price or a day is skipped; Catalyst bond prices stay a percent of nominal until Stage 3; a symbol is Bossa's short (`CDR`) or full (`CDPROJEKT`) one, the reference price stands in when there was no trade, and a non-PLN Instrument is a `PriceSourceError`. Tests run on trimmed responses in `tests/fixtures/bossa/`.
- `sources/analizy.py`: `AnalizySource(fetch).quote(symbol, currency)`, funds and PPK from the undocumented analizy.pl API (`/api/quotation/fio/<ID>`): the newest valuation of the first series with its day (often D-1), in the fund's currency (a mismatch is a `PriceSourceError`). `sources/bankier.py`: `BankierSource`, its fallback, reads the price and day from the header (`data-symbol="<ID>"`) of the bankier.pl fund page, then the PPK page. Tests run on `tests/fixtures/analizy/` and trimmed pages in `tests/fixtures/bankier/`. The Instrument dialog has "Symbol Yahoo", "Symbol Bossa", "Symbol analizy.pl" and "Symbol bankier.pl"; `build_shell` defaults to all four sources.
- `sources/prices.py`: the `PriceSource` protocol and `refresh_prices(engine, sources, now)`: every Instrument with an open Position, its sources tried in order, the first Quote saved (`persistence/quotes.py` `save_quote`, one per Instrument and day, `latest_quotes`), returning a `RefreshResult` with the Instruments that failed in every source (reasons per source). Each refresh replaces the saved failures (`persistence/quotes.py` `save_refresh` and `load_refresh`, table `price_failures`, migration 0013; the time in `app_settings`); a source raising anything else than `PriceSourceError` counts as that source failing, so `last_refresh` gives the header's "Wyceny: <time>" status, red with the error count and clickable for the reasons, and the Portfolio marks those Instruments' Quotes stale. The header's refresh button runs it through `run.io_bound`; `build_shell(engine, rates, sources)` takes test sources.
- `sources/stooq.py`: `StooqSource(api_key, fetch).history(symbol, currency, first, last)`, daily closes from the Stooq CSV download (history and indices only, never current prices; the closes are taken as in the Instrument's quote currency). It needs the user's personal API key (`persistence/settings.py` `STOOQ_API_KEY` in `app_settings`, set in Ustawienia > Źródła wycen, `ui/price_sources.py`); `fetch_from_stooq` first answers Stooq's browser check (`answer_challenge`, a SHA-256 proof of work) and keeps the cookie. Tests run on `tests/fixtures/stooq/` (illustrative CSVs in the documented layout until a real response is saved). `sources/history.py` `QuoteHistory(engine).between(instrument_id, first, last)` is "an Instrument's Quotes in a date range" for Stage 4: days not fetched yet are fetched once from Stooq (up to yesterday; today belongs to the price refresh) into the same `quotes` table, then read from the cache; an Instrument without a Stooq Source Symbol gives only its saved Quotes. `domain/history.py` `Cover` and `days_to_fetch` keep the fetched days one range (`persistence/history.py`, tables `quote_history`, `nbp_rate_history` and `nbp_history`, migration 0014); a new Stooq symbol, deleting the Instrument or clearing the portfolio drops its history. An index (e.g. WIG) is an Instrument with only a Stooq symbol. `NbpRates.history(currency, first, last)` gives the NBP Rates published in a range the same way (93-day pieces, from 2002-01-02, up to yesterday).
- `domain/instruments.py`: `AssetClass`, `asset_class_name` validation and `InstrumentDraft` (name, Asset Class id, quote currency, market, optional Manual Price), raising `InstrumentError`. `persistence/instruments.py` saves both (`list_asset_classes`, `add_asset_class`, `rename_asset_class`, `list_instruments`, `add_instrument`, `update_instrument`). Instruments point at their Asset Class by id, so a rename shows everywhere; migration 0004 seeds the sheet's 12 classes. `domain/currencies.py` holds the shared currency-code check, `NbpRate` and `MissingNbpRateError`.
- `sources/nbp.py`: `NbpRates.before(currency, day)` gives the NBP Rate (D-1, table A) from the NBP API, cached per currency and day in SQLite (`persistence/nbp_rates.py`, so `sources/` sits above `persistence/`), and `latest(currency)` values foreign Positions until automatic valuation (#3); a missing rate raises `MissingNbpRateError` and blocks the save. Its `fetch` and `today` are injectable, so tests run on saved responses in `tests/fixtures/nbp/` without the network; `build_shell(engine, rates)` takes a test instance.
- `persistence/reset.py`: `clear_portfolio(engine)` backs the database up into the `backups` folder next to it (no backup, nothing cleared: `BackupError`), then removes every Account, Instrument (with its Source Symbols, Quotes and refresh failures) and Transaction and restores `domain/instruments.py` `DEFAULT_ASSET_CLASSES` (the NBP Rate cache and `app_settings` stay); `portfolio_counts` feeds the warning. `ui/reset.py` is its card at the bottom of Ustawienia: a warning with the counts, then the typed phrase `USUŃ WSZYSTKO` enables the final button.
- `domain/export.py` builds the Transakcje rows for export (every type, the Actual and Tax Amounts, origin, comment); `sources/export.py` writes them to CSV (UTF-8 with BOM, `;`, decimal comma, day-first dates) or XLSX. The Transakcje tab's "Eksportuj CSV" and "Eksportuj XLSX" buttons hand the file to the browser.
- `ui/shell.py`: the NiceGUI window (left menu, header with the View Scope switcher, "+" transaction button). Tabs are `ui.sub_pages` routes; `build_shell(engine)` hands the engine to the pages that need the database (`ui/settings.py` Ustawienia with `ui/accounts.py` Konta and `ui/instruments.py` Klasy aktywów and Instrumenty, `ui/transactions.py` Transakcje tab and the transaction dialog, opened empty by "+" or filled by clicking a listed Transaction to edit or delete it; a foreign-currency trade offers the payment source ("Płatność") on an Account holding the currency and the "Prowizja <broker> (przewalutowanie X%)" checkbox on an Account with an FX Conversion Fee, `ui/portfolio.py` read-only Portfolio: an expandable card per Account with Cash Balance and Positions at the Manual Price, foreign ones converted at the NBP Rate; clicking a Position shows its Lots with both costs; foreign cash with its cost, value and the Account's FX result, left out of the Account's result when the Account excludes it; and the Account's net Dividends and its commissions and costs). After a save, the dialog's `on_saved` callback refreshes the tab on screen.
- `app.py`: entry point, also used by `main.py` and by the console-less `financial-app-gui.exe` that the desktop shortcut starts (`scripts/create_desktop_shortcut.ps1`). Without a console, stdout/stderr go to `paths.log_path()`.

The app re-implements the inwestomat.eu "Portfolio tracker" Google Sheet. The agreed spec is `docs/spec.md`, the glossary is `CONTEXT.md` and key decisions are in `docs/adr/`. `reference/` holds the user's spreadsheet export and its Apps Script source; it is git-ignored because it contains personal financial data, so never commit it or copy its data into tracked files.

## Environment

- Python 3.14 in a virtualenv at `.venv/`. Dependencies are listed in `pyproject.toml`; the package is installed in editable mode with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pip install -e ".[dev]"`.
- Windows host. Use the venv interpreter directly, without relying on activation:
  - Run the app (native window): `.venv/Scripts/python.exe main.py`
  - Tests: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest` (UI tests use NiceGUI's `user_simulation`, with no browser)
  - Type check: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m mypy` (strict)
  - Lint and format: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m ruff check .` and `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m ruff format .`
