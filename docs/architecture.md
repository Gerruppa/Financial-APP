# Architecture by module

What each module of `src/financial_app/` does, its main functions and UI markers, and the issue or spec section it comes from. `CLAUDE.md` points here; update the entry of a module you change.

Layout (`src/financial_app/`, see ADR-0001):
- `domain/`: pure-Python calculations. It must never import NiceGUI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic or pywebview; `tests/test_architecture.py` enforces this. `domain/formatting.py` is the single Polish number and date formatter (`format_pln`, `format_percent`, `format_quantity`, `format_unit_price`, `format_date`, `format_datetime`, `format_count` (Polish plurals), `format_exact` for edit fields, `format_exact_percent` for a stored percent like a TER and `format_points` for percentage points) and parser of typed input (`parse_number`, `parse_date`); use it for every amount or date shown in or read from the UI.
- `persistence/`: SQLite through SQLAlchemy. `models.py` holds the ORM models, `migrations/` holds Alembic revisions, and `db.init_db(path)` applies them at start-up (`db.migrate(engine)` alone, e.g. on a test's in-memory database), right after `backup.backup_database` copies the file into `paths.backups_dir()` (newest 30 kept). The database lives in the user's app-data folder (`paths.data_dir()`, overridable with `FINANCIAL_APP_DATA_DIR`), never in the repo. Change the schema only through a new migration: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m alembic revision --autogenerate -m "..."`. `tests/test_database.py` fails if the models and migrations drift apart.
- `domain/accounts.py`: Account Types (Bonds and Other, #64, are stored as text, so new types need no migration), the Tax Regime registry (`tax_regime_for`; OKI is a placeholder, Other only describes the 6-month rule for selling things) and `AccountDraft`, which validates Account fields and raises `AccountError` with a Polish message. `persistence/accounts.py` saves Accounts (`list_accounts`, `add_account`, `update_account`) and enforces case-insensitive unique names. `delete_account` (L-28, #103) deletes an Account only while `account_holders` finds nothing naming it (`domain/accounts.py` `AccountHolders`: its Transactions, Transfers into it, its own Strategy, XTB account numbers mapped to it; `refusal` gives the Polish message) and forgets a View Scope on it; the Account dialog's `delete-account` (editing only, with `on_deleted`) shows the refusal or asks `confirm-delete-account`, and the shell's `account_deleted` switches a scope on it back to Total.
- `domain/spreadsheet_import.py`: reads the Transakcje sheet's columns, classifies tickers (`EXCH:SYM`, bonds `TYP-DDMMRR` → Bond Series, `Gotówka`, `Waluty_<CUR>`), builds the Transaction for each row (Actual Amount = the sheet's "Total PLN") and fingerprints rows so a re-import skips them.
- `sources/spreadsheet.py`: opens the workbook (openpyxl), `preview_import` (matches Konta and Instruments, the user's `Decisions` resolve the rest, nothing saved) and `save_import` (creates the new Instruments and saves every row or none). `ui/import_sheet.py` is its section in Ustawienia; an unmatched Konto can create an Account through `ui/accounts.py` `open_account_dialog` (#63), prefilled by `cash_currencies_of`. Transactions keep their Origin (`domain/transactions.py` `Origin`, migration 0011 adds `origin` and `external_id`).
- XTB import and Reconciliation (L-17, spec 7): `domain/xtb_import.py` reads the "Cash Operations" sheet of xStation's export (`parse_statement` → `XtbStatement`, the account number from its first row; `warsaw_day` dates UTC times without tzdata) into `XtbEntry`s (`xtb_entries`: Buys and Sells from the comment, parts of one order merged, interest with its month's tax, transfers paired across the statements imported together into a `TRANSFER`, else a Deposit or Withdrawal, the rest `UNSUPPORTED`; `external_id` "xtb-" + the lowest cash row id, a transfer's both legs; `unresolved` leaves only the leg not imported before), `xtb_draft` (Origin `BROKER`; a foreign trade a `BrokerConversion` of the PLN XTB charged), `yahoo_symbol` and `match_instrument`. `domain/reconciliation.py` `reconcile` pairs broker drafts with the entered Transactions (Account, type, Instrument, quantity, target, ±`DATE_WINDOW` days, amount within 1% without an Instrument) and the `incoming` Cash Transfers into its Accounts (paired with a broker Deposit or Withdrawal as `linked`, always kept) into `Pair`s (`same`: date, amount, price, commission, tax), broker-only and entered-only; `Choice` BROKER (default) or MINE, kept by `item_key`. `persistence/reconciliation.py` `apply_reconciliation` applies `AddBroker`, `TakeBroker`, `KeepMine`, `DeleteMine`, `SkipBroker` all or none and replays the touched Positions; `handled_broker_ids`, `unreconciled_transactions` (within the statement's days, only `RECONCILED_TYPES`, no foreign Dividends) and `incoming_transfers` read `transactions.broker_id`, `reconciled_on` and the `skipped_broker_rows` table (migration 0026). `sources/xtb.py` (`read_statements` of a file or folder, `preview_xtb`, `apply_xtb`) refuses a non-PLN XTB account, matches accounts (the one IKE/IKZE Account suggested) and tickers, remembered in `app_settings` under `persistence/settings.py` `XTB_ACCOUNT`/`XTB_TICKER` (cleared by `clear_portfolio`). `ui/xtb_import.py` `XtbImportSection` is its card in Ustawienia (`xtb-path`, `xtb-load`, `xtb-account-<number>`, `xtb-instrument-<ticker>` whose "+ Nowy instrument…" opens `InstrumentsSection.open_dialog(None, query=, on_saved=)`, `xtb-summary`, `xtb-all-broker`/`xtb-all-mine`, toggles `xtb-choice-<key>`, `xtb-apply`). `tests/test_xtb_parity.py` checks the user's exports in `reference/` (units against "Open Positions", cash against the total); tests build statements with `tests/xtb_rows.py`.
- `domain/transactions.py`: Transaction Types (Deposit, Withdrawal, Buy, Sell, Dividend, Cost, Currency Exchange, DRIP, Split, Cash Transfer, Security Transfer so far), `TransactionDraft` validation, `buy_or_sell(...)` (builds a Buy/Sell and derives its Actual Amount; a foreign-currency one carries the user's optional `fx_rate` and the `NbpRate`, from which the `tax_amount` property follows, or a `BrokerConversion` (the PLN the broker charged and the Account's FX Conversion Fee %): the trade stores the percent, and its `fx_conversion_fee` and `effective_fx_rate` follow, the fee counting as a cost in the Tax Amount; `cash_currency` says whether it is paid from PLN or from foreign cash of the Instrument's currency, the commission always in PLN), `currency_exchange(...)` (PLN to a foreign Cash Currency or back, `to_pln`), `dividend(...)` (gross and withholding tax in the Instrument's or the chosen currency, the net amount into PLN at the user's rate or the NBP Rate, or the PLN the broker credited with a `BrokerConversion` (its fee a cost), or into foreign cash of that currency; no Instrument means interest), `cost(...)` (PLN, or from foreign cash valued at the NBP Rate), `drip(...)` (a Dividend reinvested into `quantity` units: a Lot costing the net amount, at the NBP Rate if foreign, no cash moving), `split(...)` (a `SplitRatio` X:Y, no amount), `cash_transfer(...)` (PLN to `target_account_id`, which `cash_balances` credits) and `security_transfer(...)` (units to `target_account_id`, no amount), `costs_by_account` (Costs, commissions and FX Conversion Fees) and `dividends_by_account`, `covering_exchange(buy)` (the "wymień walutę automatycznie" Exchange buying exactly the Buy's foreign amount), `cash_balances` (per Account, in any currency) and `lowest_cash_balance` (drives the insufficient-cash warning, per currency). `persistence/transactions.py` saves them (`add_transaction`, `add_transactions` (several at once, all or none), `update_transaction` and `delete_transaction`, each replaying the touched Positions to block a change that would leave a Sell or Security Transfer uncovered or a Split before the first Buy; `list_transactions`, newest first, for the Transakcje list; `transactions_in_entry_order`, by id, for the FIFO engine and everything replaying it: no caller reverses `list_transactions`).
- `domain/lots.py`: the single FIFO engine (ADR-0003). `open_positions(transactions)` takes Transactions in entry order, sorts by date and the same-day order, and returns `Position`s (open `Lot`s with Actual `cost` and `tax_cost`, average price, value and result at a PLN price); a Split rescales open Lots (units × X/Y, costs kept) and a Security Transfer moves the oldest Lots to the target Account with their dates and costs; an uncovered Sell or Security Transfer raises `InsufficientQuantityError`, a Split before the Account held the Instrument `SplitBeforeFirstBuyError`. Same-day order: Split, Buy, Security Transfer, Sell, DRIP. `foreign_cash(transactions)` holds foreign cash as Lots per Account and currency (`ForeignCash`, with the realised FX result; spending more than held only warns and leaves a negative Lot), and `fx_result` sums an Account's realised and unrealised FX result.
- `domain/valuation.py`: `instrument_price(instrument, quote, rate_for)` decides the price a Position is valued at and its `PriceStatus`: the Manual Price while it is set (carrying the newest Quote to show beside it), else the newest Quote, else no price; no NBP Rate to convert it is its own status, and the rate is asked for only when there is a price. Its `Badge` (ręczna, nieaktualna when `stale`, bez źródła) shows beside the Position. A physical metal (`InstrumentDraft.metal_grams`, the metal weight of one unit, and `buyback_spread_percent`, migration 0015) has its prices (Manual Price and Quotes) as the spot of a troy ounce (`GRAMS_PER_TROY_OUNCE`): `unit_price` scales it to the unit less the spread (`unit_spot` without it), and `InstrumentPrice.spot` and `unit_spot_pln` keep the spot to show beside it. The Portfolio only displays it.
- `domain/prices.py`: `Quote`, `PriceSourceError` (Polish message), the Price Source keys (`YAHOO`, `BOSSA`, ...) and `SOURCE_NAMES`, and `sources_to_try(fallback_order, source_symbols)`: the Asset Class's Fallback Order, keeping only the sources the Instrument has a Source Symbol for (`symbol_in`: bankier.pl falls back to the Fund Code, the analizy.pl Source Symbol, #58; `without_fund_code_copy` drops a bankier.pl symbol equal to it, applied when the dialog opens and saves). Each Asset Class stores its own order (#41, `AssetClass.fallback_order`, copied onto `Instrument.fallback_order`; column `asset_classes.fallback_order`, comma-separated keys, migration 0018): `default_fallback_order(name)` gives the spec 4.1 defaults (any other class gets that of "Inne"), `validate_fallback_order` allows each of `FALLBACK_SOURCES` once (`FallbackOrderError`), `persistence/instruments.py` `set_fallback_order` saves it, `add_asset_class` gives a new class the current order of "Inne" (`new_class_fallback_order`) unless given one, and `clear_portfolio` restores the defaults. Ustawienia > Klasy aktywów shows each order and edits it in the class dialog (move, remove, add a source); `AssetClassesSection.on_changed` redraws the Instruments and the unpriced list. Instruments carry `source_symbols` (source key to symbol) in `InstrumentDraft`, saved in `source_symbols` (migration 0012).
- `sources/yahoo.py`: `YahooSource(fetch).quote(symbol, currency)`, from the chart API: the price in the Instrument's quote currency (GBp ÷ 100; crypto in another currency through Yahoo's own `XXXYYY=X` rate; any other mismatch is a `PriceSourceError`), on the exchange's day (`regularMarketTime` + `gmtoffset`). Tests run on `tests/fixtures/yahoo/`.
- `sources/bossa.py`: `BossaSource(fetch, bonds).quote(symbol, "PLN")` from the Bossa JSON API (GPW shares, NewConnect, ETF and Catalyst bonds, searched in that order): one request per category per refresh (`refresh_prices` calls each source's `new_refresh` first, and a failed category is not asked again in that refresh); a listing without a positive price or a day is skipped; a Catalyst bond's percent of nominal becomes a price per bond (`domain/catalyst.py` `bond_price`: percent × nominal / 100 + accrued interest, from `bonds`, a `BondFactsSource`, by default `sources/catalyst.py` `CatalystFacts`), and without its nominal it gets no Quote; a symbol is Bossa's short (`CDR`) or full (`CDPROJEKT`) one, the reference price stands in when there was no trade, and a non-PLN Instrument is a `CurrencyMismatchError` (the symbol is looked up first, so the check can show its PLN Quote). Tests run on trimmed responses in `tests/fixtures/bossa/`.
- Catalyst bonds (L-23, L-24, spec 4.2): `domain/catalyst.py` (`BondFacts` nominal and accrued interest per bond in PLN, `bond_price`, `is_indexed` for `IZ_SERIES`, `IndexationDay` and `indexed_facts`: 1000 zł × the day's coefficient, rounded half up, the last day before it standing in up to `INDEXATION_STANDS_IN`, a week). `sources/catalyst.py` `CatalystFacts(fetch).of(symbol, day)` (`day` the Quote's) reads `parse_catalyst_listing` (the gpwcatalyst.pl "Instrumenty notowane" PLN table) and, for an IZ bond first, `parse_indexation` (the gov.pl "Współczynniki indeksacji" table), each page once a refresh (`new_refresh`), raising `PriceSourceError` without them. The sheet import reads the optional "Cena nominalna" column (`OPTIONAL_COLUMNS`; `SheetLine.unit_price` turns a percent into a price per bond when the nominal is over 1, the fingerprint unchanged). Migration 0025 gives "Obligacje skarbowe polskie" the order Bossa unless chosen and drops the Bossa Quotes of the Polish bond classes saved as percents. Tests run on `tests/fixtures/catalyst/` and `tests/fixtures/indexation/` and the bond listings in `tests/fixtures/bossa/_cat_bonds.json`.
- `sources/analizy.py`: `AnalizySource(fetch).quote(symbol, currency)`, funds and PPK from the undocumented analizy.pl API (`/api/quotation/fio/<ID>`): the newest valuation of the first series with its day (often D-1), in the fund's currency (a mismatch is a `PriceSourceError`). `sources/bankier.py`: `BankierSource`, its fallback, reads the price and day from the header (`data-symbol="<ID>"`) of the bankier.pl fund page, then the PPK page. Tests run on `tests/fixtures/analizy/` and trimmed pages in `tests/fixtures/bankier/`. `AnalizySearch(fetch)` (#60) finds FIO, SFIO, PPK and FZG funds through the site's search API (exact `fields` parameter), names without markup or the former name, currency from each fund's quotation, PPK in Multi-asset; tests on `tests/fixtures/analizy/search/`. The Instrument dialog has "Symbol Yahoo", "Symbol Bossa", "Kod funduszu" (the analizy.pl field, standing where the first of analizy.pl and bankier.pl is in the class's order; its "Sprawdź" checks both) and "Symbol bankier.pl (gdy inny niż kod funduszu)", always under "Więcej źródeł" (#57: the chosen Asset Class's Fallback Order sources first, in that order, marker `class-sources`; the rest in the collapsed "Więcej źródeł (N wypełnione)", `more-sources`, still saved; Stooq in "Historia notowań (Stooq)", `stooq-history`; no class or an empty order shows a note where the price comes from; a class change moves the same fields, so values and checks stay); `build_shell` defaults to all four sources and to `CombinedSearch` of Yahoo and analizy.pl. Each of those fields has a "Sprawdź" button (#39): `sources/prices.py` `check_symbol(source, symbol, currency)` fetches one Quote anew (`new_refresh` first) and returns a `SymbolCheck` (the Quote, a `warning` or an `error`), shown under the field as "<price> <currency> · <day>"; nothing is saved, so the Instrument saves whatever the check found. A source quoting in another currency than the Instrument's raises `domain/prices.py` `CurrencyMismatchError` (a `PriceSourceError` carrying the source's own Quote), which the check shows as a warning beside that price.
- `domain/instrument_search.py`: the Instrument search (#25): `SearchResult` (a listing with its market, quote currency, the Source Symbols it gives and the ISIN searched for; Yahoo search returns no ISINs, so only an ISIN query fills one), the swappable `InstrumentSearch` protocol raising `InstrumentSearchError`, and `fill_empty(FormFields, result)`, which fills only the empty form fields (name, ISIN, quote currency, market, Source Symbols, Asset Class name, and for a `fund` result the domicile from the ISIN's country, `DOMICILES`, and the distribution policy from Acc/Dist words in its name), never overwriting what is entered; the dialog sets a class only while none is chosen, matched by name ignoring case (#59). `CombinedSearch` (#60) asks several searches side by side, marks each result's `source` and returns `SearchResults` whose `notes` say "<źródło> nie odpowiada"; all failing is an `InstrumentSearchError`. `sources/yahoo.py` `YahooSearch(fetch).search(query)` asks Yahoo search, plus one chart request per listing for its currency (in parallel); a `.WA` listing also gives the Bossa symbol; `quoteType` gives the Asset Class (EQUITY: Akcje polskie on `.WA`, else Akcje zagraniczne; CRYPTOCURRENCY, FUTURE, MUTUALFUND; ETF none); Yahoo matches an ISIN to one listing, so the listings sharing its full name are added. The Instrument dialog has the "Szukaj (ISIN, nazwa lub symbol)" field (prefilled with the name of an Instrument without Source Symbols; a new Instrument's default PLN counts as empty) and an "ISIN" field; `InstrumentDraft.isin` is optional and not unique (migration 0016). `build_shell(..., search=...)` takes a test search. Tests run on `tests/fixtures/yahoo/search/`.
- `sources/prices.py`: the `PriceSource` protocol and `refresh_prices(engine, sources, now)`: every Instrument with an open Position, its sources tried in order, the first Quote saved (`persistence/quotes.py` `save_quote`, one per Instrument and day, `latest_quotes`), returning a `RefreshResult` with the Instruments that failed in every source (reasons per source). Each refresh replaces the saved failures (`persistence/quotes.py` `save_refresh` and `load_refresh`, table `price_failures`, migration 0013; the time in `app_settings`); a source raising anything else than `PriceSourceError` counts as that source failing, so `last_refresh` gives the header's "Wyceny: <time>" status, red with the error count and clickable for the reasons, and the Portfolio marks those Instruments' Quotes stale. A refresh is a success (`RefreshResult.succeeded`; its time in `persistence/quotes.py` `last_successful_refresh`, app setting `prices_succeeded_at`) unless every Instrument fetched failed. The header's refresh button runs it through `run.io_bound`, and so does the start-up refresh (`app.py` passes `build_shell(..., refresh_at_start=True)`) when `domain/prices.py` `refresh_due` says the last success was before the most recent weekday `SESSION_CLOSE` (17:30, no holiday calendar); meanwhile the header says "Wyceny: odświeżanie…", and a reloaded window joins the running refresh (`_running_refreshes`, one per database) instead of starting another. `build_shell(engine, rates, sources, refresh_at_start, now)` takes test sources and a clock.
- `domain/valuation.py` `instruments_without_price` and `sources/prices.py` `unpriced_instruments(engine)` give the "Instrumenty bez wyceny" list (#40): held Instruments, Bond Series aside, with no Source Symbol in their Fallback Order (`UnpricedReason.NO_SOURCE`), failing in every source in the last refresh (`FAILED`, with the reasons, until the next refresh) or with a symbol but no Quote yet (`NOT_REFRESHED`). `ui/unpriced.py` `UnpricedSection` is its card in Ustawienia below Instrumenty; a row opens the Instrument dialog. The shell keeps the section and redraws it after a price refresh or a Transaction save, and `InstrumentsSection.refresh` redraws it through `on_changed`.
- `sources/stooq.py`: `StooqSource(api_key, fetch).history(symbol, currency, first, last)`, daily closes from the Stooq CSV download (history and indices only, never current prices; the closes are taken as in the Instrument's quote currency). It needs the user's personal API key (`persistence/settings.py` `STOOQ_API_KEY` in `app_settings`, set in Ustawienia > Źródła wycen, `ui/price_sources.py`); `fetch_from_stooq` first answers Stooq's browser check (`answer_challenge`, a SHA-256 proof of work) and keeps the cookie. Tests run on `tests/fixtures/stooq/` (illustrative CSVs in the documented layout until a real response is saved). `sources/history.py` `QuoteHistory(engine).between(instrument_id, first, last)` is "an Instrument's Quotes in a date range" for Stage 4: days not fetched yet are fetched once from Yahoo, else analizy.pl (`AnalizySource.history`, the fund's whole valuation series filtered to the days, saved as `ANALIZY_HISTORY`, #101), else Stooq (#68; up to yesterday; today belongs to the price refresh) into the same `quotes` table, only on days without a Quote, then read from the cache (the last `UNSETTLED_DAYS` are asked again until Stooq has a Quote on or after them; `latest_quotes` never takes a Stooq, `YAHOO_HISTORY` or `ANALIZY_HISTORY` Quote, so history never values a Position; only PLN Instruments); an Instrument without a Stooq Source Symbol gives only its saved Quotes. `domain/history.py` `Cover` and `days_to_fetch` keep the fetched days one range (`persistence/history.py`, tables `quote_history`, `nbp_rate_history` and `nbp_history`, migration 0014); a new Stooq symbol, deleting the Instrument or clearing the portfolio drops its history. An index (e.g. WIG) is an Instrument with only a Stooq symbol. `NbpRates.history(currency, first, last)` gives the NBP Rates published in a range the same way (93-day pieces, from 2002-01-02, up to yesterday).
- `domain/portfolio_history.py` (#68, spec 6.1, ADR-0006): `daily_history(transactions, instruments, prices, rates, first, last)` gives a `DayValue` per day (value per Account and Asset Class, PLN cash in `CASH_CLASS`, foreign cash in `FOREIGN_CASH_CLASS`; `net_deposits` from Deposits and Withdrawals, `account_net_deposits` also from Cash and Security Transfers, the latter at that day's value, else the moved Lots' cost; `unpriced` Instruments valued at cost). Positions come from `open_positions` replayed up to each day; prices and NBP Rates (published on day D, not D-1) carry forward; Bond Series stay at cost. A `HistoryPrice` with `adjusted_on` (from a history source) is in that day's units, so it is rescaled by the Splits recorded in between. `YahooSource.history` gives Yahoo's daily `close` (never `adjclose`), saved as `YAHOO_HISTORY`; `QuoteHistory` tries `HISTORY_SOURCES` (Yahoo, analizy.pl, then Stooq; one fixed order for now, not per Asset Class) and `latest_quotes` ignores `HISTORY_ONLY_SOURCES`; only the `SPLIT_ADJUSTED_SOURCES` (Stooq, Yahoo history) carry `adjusted_on`. `sources/portfolio_history.py` `PortfolioHistory`: `fetch(progress)` fills the cache from each Instrument's and currency's first Transaction to yesterday (failures returned, not raised), `values()` computes from the cache alone through `run.cpu_bound` (injectable) and keeps it until `invalidate()`. `values()` ignores a computation overtaken by an `invalidate()`. The shell wires it through `ui/history.py` (#71): `SharedHistory` per database (shared by its windows) fetches in `run.io_bound` after the start-up price refresh (only with `refresh_at_start`) with the header status "Historia: pobieranie… (n/N)", then red "Historia: n błędów" listing the failures (`history-status`); a Transaction save or price refresh invalidates it and recomputes in the background; `build_shell(..., history=...)` takes a test instance (tests pass an inline `compute`).
- `domain/metrics.py` (#69, spec 6.1): on a `daily_history` list, a period (`first`, `last`, clipped to the History) and a scope (`account_id`, `None` for Total), `xirr` (the "from" day's value paid in, each day's change in Net Deposits as a flow, the "to" value paid out; `None` without a rate), `twr` (daily (V_D − F_D) / V_{D−1} − 1 chained, flows at the end of the day, a day after a zero value skipped), `drawdowns` (per day, on the TWR), `max_drawdown` (`MaxDrawdown`: depth, peak, trough, `recovered_on`, from which Wyniki counts the recovery days) and `volatility` (sample deviation of weekday TWR returns × √252). Returns are fractions, not percent. `daily_change` (V_today − V_yesterday − today's flows) feeds the View Scope switcher; the Dashboard charts use `drawdowns` and `xirr_to_each`, Wyniki the rest (`domain/results.py`).
- View Scope and period selector (#71, spec 5 and 6.1): `domain/view_scope.py` (`parse_scope`/`scope_setting`, text `total` or an Account id in `app_settings` key `view_scope`, a gone Account falls back to Total; `scope_entries` sorts Accounts by History's value today with share and daily change, by name while History loads) and `ui/view_scope.py` (`ViewScope` with `on_change` listeners and `includes(transaction)`, `ScopeSwitcher` in the header: a menu drawn only while open (`scope-menu`, rows `scope-total`/`scope-<id>`), Total first, inactive Accounts behind "pokaż nieaktywne", "—" while History loads; `breadcrumbs` "Total › Account"). Portfolio shows only the scope's card, Transakcje only its Transactions (Transfers to it included, `domain/view_scope.py` `in_scope`), and its export buttons export that list, the file named after the Account (`transakcje-<Account>.csv`). `domain/periods.py` (`PeriodPreset` 1M…5L, Od początku, Własny; `period_dates` clips to the first Transaction; YTD starts on 31.12 of the previous year; text in `app_settings` key `period`) and `ui/periods.py` (`PeriodChoice`, `period_selector`, preset buttons `period-<key>`), shown on the Dashboard and Wyniki.
- Dashboard (#72, spec 5 and 6.1): `domain/dashboard.py` `dashboard(transactions, accounts, instruments, prices, rates, history, account_id, period)` gives the View Scope's `Totals` (Net Deposits from History's last day, `None` while it is computed; value = PLN `cash` + `open_positions`, which hold the Positions and the foreign cash as in the sheet; `unrealized` and `realized` with the FX result unless the Account excludes it; Dividends, costs and `xirr`), an `AccountRow` per Account (an inactive one only while it holds something) and the `CurrencyShare`s (Positions by quote currency plus each currency's cash). XIRR ends at today's value as the Dashboard shows it (Manual Prices included), History supplying the days before. `domain/lots.py` `realized_results` gives each Account's realised result of Sells from the same FIFO replay as `open_positions`. - `domain/export.py` builds the Transakcje rows for export (every type, the Actual and Tax Amounts, origin, comment); `sources/export.py` writes them to CSV (UTF-8 with BOM, `;`, decimal comma, day-first dates) or XLSX. The Transakcje tab's "Eksportuj CSV" and "Eksportuj XLSX" buttons hand the file to the browser.
- `ui/shell.py`: the NiceGUI window (left menu, header with the View Scope switcher, "+" transaction button `add-transaction`, hidden on Ustawienia (`SETTINGS_PATH`, L-28)). Tabs are `ui.sub_pages` routes; `build_shell(engine)` hands the engine to the pages that need the database (`ui/settings.py` Ustawienia with `ui/accounts.py` Konta and `ui/instruments.py` Klasy aktywów and Instrumenty, `ui/transactions.py` Transakcje tab and the transaction dialog, opened empty by "+" or filled by clicking a listed Transaction to edit or delete it; the Account (`transaction-account`, filled light blue with a bold name, #118) comes before the type (`transaction-type`); a foreign-currency trade offers the payment source ("Płatność") on an Account holding the currency and the "Prowizja <broker> (przewalutowanie X%)" checkbox on an Account with an FX Conversion Fee, `ui/portfolio.py` read-only Portfolio from `todays_view`: an expandable card per Account with Cash Balance and Positions at the Manual Price, foreign ones converted at the NBP Rate; clicking a Position shows its Lots with both costs; foreign cash with its cost, value and the Account's FX result, left out of the Account's result when the Account excludes it; and the Account's net Dividends and its commissions and costs). After a save, the dialog's `on_saved` callback refreshes the tab on screen.
