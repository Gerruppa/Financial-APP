# Saving Transactions (#110)

Every place that saves them goes through `sources/transaction_saving.py`: `checked_save(engine, write)` runs any all-or-none save (the sheet import's `save_import`, the XTB Reconciliation's `apply_xtb`, a bond Draft's `confirm_draft` or `confirm_rollover`) between two reads of all the Transactions and Instruments and returns `Saved` (the save's value and its `SaveWarnings`); `save_transaction(engine, draft_for, edited=, series=, with_exchange=)` is the transaction dialog's add or edit (the Bond Series `series` created on its first purchase and removed again if the save fails, the Series an edited purchase left removed once unused, the "wymień walutę automatycznie" Exchange first) and `delete_saved` its delete (its value: the Transactions left), `restore_saved(engine, transaction, dropping=)` the undo of that delete and `since_deleted` what changed before it (see `undo.md`). `domain/save_warnings.py` `save_warnings(before, after, accounts, instruments before and after, limits)` compares them: `touched` (each added, changed or removed Transaction, both forms of a change) gives the Contribution Limit bars just taken over (`over_limit`), the negative Cash Balances per Account and currency from the earliest touched day (a Cash Transfer's target in its currency, #125; `CashShortfall`, `lowest_cash_balance`; insufficient cash only warns, spec 3.3), the Multi-asset Instruments a Buy or DRIP bought without a split, the Bond Series created or removed, and the Instruments a Buy, DRIP or Security Transfer (to its target) put on an Account that may not hold them (`NotAllowed`, `_not_allowed`, with the words of `account_rules.refusal`; the dialog refuses such a Buy, so this warns of imports and confirmed Drafts); `SaveWarnings.messages(done, unsplit=)` gives the Polish texts, which `ui/save_warnings.py` `show_warnings(warnings, done, engine=, on_saved=, unsplit=)` notifies. The dialog opens the split dialog instead of naming the Instrument. Tests: `tests/test_save_warnings_domain.py`, `tests/test_transaction_saving.py` (in-memory SQLite).

The negative cash warning's fix (#267): a PLN shortfall also offers its covering Deposits, wherever the save came from (every caller of `show_warnings` passes its engine and `on_saved`, required keywords so none forgets). `SaveWarnings.short_of_pln` is the ids of the Accounts whose PLN cash went below zero (`CashShortfall.account_id`); a foreign shortfall only warns, as `covering_deposits` is PLN only. `show_warnings` then shows the bar `covering-bar` ("Brakuje gotówki w PLN.", button `covering-button` "Dodaj wpłaty uzupełniające") for `COVERING_SECONDS` (20), through `ui/undo.py` `action_bar` (the undo bar's pill, `.k-covering` in `ui/theme.py` 52px above `.k-undo`). The button opens the window `covering-window`: `sources/transaction_saving.py` `covering_proposals(engine, account_ids)`, computed on opening over all the saved Transactions (so it also covers an older shortfall the warning, checked from the touched day, did not name), is `domain/transactions.py` `covering_deposits` per Account (a PLN Deposit on each day the end-of-day Cash Balance would go below zero, of the amount it lacks, comment `domain/transactions.py` `COVERING_COMMENT`, its default, which the crypto import's Deposits share, so one search finds them all and an undone delete tells them apart), only those needing any; `deposit_choices` (shared with the crypto import's step 4) draws one checkbox per Account (`covering-account-<id>`, ticked) with its Deposits beneath, and "Zapisz wpłaty" (`covering-save`) saves the ticked ones through `save_covering` (`checked_save` over `add_transactions`, all or none), notifies its own warnings (e.g. an IKE limit) and runs `on_saved`. Once saved, the Account's lowest Cash Balance is zero. Tests: `tests/test_covering_deposits_ui.py`.

Quantity corrections (#278, `quantity-corrections.md`): when the dialog's save, edit or delete is refused with `InsufficientQuantityError`, `save_shortfalls`/`delete_shortfalls` find the Sells it leaves uncovered and `save_corrected`/`delete_corrected` make the change with the chosen fixes through `persistence/transactions.py` `save_changes`, all or none, under `checked_save`.
