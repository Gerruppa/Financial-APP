# Saving Transactions (#110)

Every place that saves them goes through `sources/transaction_saving.py`: `checked_save(engine, write)` runs any all-or-none save (the sheet import's `save_import`, the XTB Reconciliation's `apply_xtb`, a bond Draft's `confirm_draft` or `confirm_rollover`) between two reads of all the Transactions and Instruments and returns `Saved` (the save's value and its `SaveWarnings`); `save_transaction(engine, draft_for, edited=, series=, with_exchange=)` is the transaction dialog's add or edit (the Bond Series `series` created on its first purchase and removed again if the save fails, the Series an edited purchase left removed once unused, the "wymień walutę automatycznie" Exchange first) and `delete_saved` its delete, `restore_saved` the undo of that delete (see `undo.md`). `domain/save_warnings.py` `save_warnings(before, after, accounts, instruments before and after, limits)` compares them: `touched` (each added, changed or removed Transaction, both forms of a change) gives the Contribution Limit bars just taken over (`over_limit`), the negative Cash Balances per Account and currency from the earliest touched day (a Cash Transfer's target in its currency, #125; `CashShortfall`, `lowest_cash_balance`; insufficient cash only warns, spec 3.3), the Multi-asset Instruments a Buy or DRIP bought without a split, the Bond Series created or removed, and the Instruments a Buy, DRIP or Security Transfer (to its target) put on an Account that may not hold them (`NotAllowed`, `_not_allowed`, with the words of `account_rules.refusal`; the dialog refuses such a Buy, so this warns of imports and confirmed Drafts); `SaveWarnings.messages(done, unsplit=)` gives the Polish texts, which `ui/save_warnings.py` `show_warnings` notifies. The dialog opens the split dialog instead of naming the Instrument. Tests: `tests/test_save_warnings_domain.py`, `tests/test_transaction_saving.py` (in-memory SQLite).
