# Dashboard (#72, spec 5 and 6.1)

`domain/dashboard.py` `dashboard(transactions, accounts, instruments, prices, rates, history, account_id, period)` gives the View Scope's `Totals` (Net Deposits from History's last day, `None` while it is computed; value = PLN `cash` + `open_positions`, which hold the Positions and the foreign cash as in the sheet; `unrealized` and `realized` with the FX result unless the Account excludes it; Dividends, costs and `xirr`), an `AccountRow` per Account (an inactive one only while it holds something) and the `CurrencyShare`s (Positions by quote currency plus each currency's cash). XIRR ends at today's value as the Dashboard shows it (Manual Prices included), History supplying the days before. `domain/lots.py` `realized_results` gives each Account's realised result of Sells from the same FIFO replay as `open_positions`. It takes today's state from `sources/todays_view.py` (below) and the price status text from `sources/prices.py` `refresh_summary`, which the header shares. `ui/dashboard.py` `DashboardPage` draws, as chosen with the prototype of #174: the main card (`dashboard-hero`) with the value (`hero-value`) and its change since the last Valuation Snapshot (`since-snapshot`) beside the period selector and `ui/charts.py` `hero_value_chart` (`chart-hero`, `chart-hero-pending` while History is computed); the price status (`dashboard-prices`) and "Po podatku"; the tiles (`total-<key>`) with their coloured icons (`TILE_ICONS`, `tile-icon-<key>`) and, for cash and costs, their share of the Portfolio (`Totals.share_of_value`, `total-<key>-note`); a row of the Investment Goal (`investment-goal`) and the saying of the day (`dashboard-saying`, a third of the row, #178), either taking the row alone; "Twoje konta i aktywa" (`account-table`, the `account-table` visibility key) as a card per Account (`account-card`: value, P&L since its first Transaction with `Totals.profit_share`, a `sparkline` of its value in the period; a click opens its View Scope) or, behind `accounts-view-table` (back with `accounts-view-cards`, not remembered), the Account table (`account-row`); and the exposure (`currency-row`). It is redrawn on a scope or period change, a save, a price refresh and when History is computed.
