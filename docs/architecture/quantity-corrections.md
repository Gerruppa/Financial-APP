# Quantity corrections (#278)

A Sell or Security Transfer taking more units FIFO than its Account held is still refused by the engine (`domain/lots.py` `InsufficientQuantityError`, ADR-0003: quantities stay exact), but the error now carries the `transaction` that took them and the `held_cost` of the units held, and the imports and the transaction dialog offer a correcting Transaction the user confirms, saved with the change all or none, the way the negative cash warning offers covering Deposits (`transaction-saving.md`).

`domain/quantity_corrections.py`: `quantity_shortfalls(transactions, fixes, trim_saved=)` replays the Transactions (in entry order), and for each uncovered FIFO Sell or Security Transfer, by date, records a `Shortfall` (`uncovered`, `held`, `held_cost`, `gap`, `value` in PLN, `large` above `LARGE_GAP` 1% of the quantity) with its `fix`: the one chosen in `fixes` (by `shortfall_key`: the import's external id, `#<id>` for a saved Transaction, `NEW` for the dialog's draft), else `Fix.FREE_BUY` for a small gap, else `None`; it then applies the fix (a `None` one as `FREE_BUY`) by identity and replays again, so a trimmed transfer brings its target fewer units and a later Sell lacks more. A Sell naming its Lot (an Early Redemption) or any other replay error ends the search. `Shortfall.fixes` is what is offered: `FREE_BUY` (a Buy of the gap on the Sell's day at price 0, `CORRECTION_COMMENT`), `PRICED_BUY` (a Sell only, at its own price and rates, if worth at least a grosz) and `TRIM` (the Transaction with the units held, a Sell keeping its amount at a price rounded to 8 decimals; not when nothing was held, nor for a saved Transaction without `trim_saved`, as an import only adds). `correction(fix)` gives the Buy added and the trimmed replacement; `effect(fix)` the Polish text of what it does to cost and realised result; `chosen_corrections(shortfalls)` the trimmed ones by key and the Buys. An imported Transaction's Buy is imported too (`Origin.BROKER`, `<external id>-correction`), so it is not offered again.

A Buy at cost 0 is an ordinary Buy at price 0 (`TransactionDraft.costs_nothing`, no migration): amount 0, no commission or broker conversion, no NBP Rate needed (`persistence/transactions.py` `check_references`); its Lot costs 0, so a Position of only such Lots shows no result percentage (`ui/portfolio.py`).

Where it is offered:

- Crypto import (`crypto-import.md`): `sources/crypto.py` `proposed_corrections(engine, previews, decisions)` over `persistence/reconciliation.py` `after_actions` (the saved Transactions as the actions would leave them) with `CryptoDecisions.corrections`; step 4 shows them (`crypto-corrections-<n>`), step 5 counts them (`crypto-corrections-summary`) and waits for every fix (`crypto-corrections-missing`, "Zastosuj" disabled); `apply_crypto` refuses an unresolved one and saves `with_corrections(actions, shortfalls)` (a trimmed draft in place of its AddBroker or TakeBroker, the Buys added last). `proposed_deposits` takes the chosen corrections into account (a priced Buy spends cash).
- XTB (`xtb-import-reconciliation.md`): `XtbPreview.shortfalls` from the chosen Reconciliation (`chosen_actions`), `XtbDecisions.corrections`; `actions()` adds the corrections, `blocked` waits for every fix; the card lists them under the Reconciliation (`xtb-corrections-<n>`).
- Transaction dialog (`transactions.md`): a save, edit or delete refused with `InsufficientQuantityError` draws the fixes below the error (`transaction-corrections`, a radio per shortfall `transaction-corrections-fix-<n>`) and a button (`transaction-save-corrected`, "Zapisz z poprawką" or "Usuń z poprawką"), disabled while a fix is missing. `sources/transaction_saving.py` `save_shortfalls`/`save_corrected` and `delete_shortfalls`/`delete_corrected` save through `persistence/transactions.py` `save_changes(added=, updated=, deleted=)`, one all-or-none session replaying the touched Positions; here a saved Sell an edit or delete uncovers may be trimmed too. A Bond Series purchase gets no corrections.

`ui/quantity_corrections.py` `correction_choices(shortfalls, describe, choose, marker)` draws them the same way everywhere (`<marker>-<n>`, the warning `<marker>-large-<n>`, the radio `<marker>-fix-<n>`), with `HINT` and `shortfall_text`.

Tests: `tests/test_quantity_corrections_domain.py`, `tests/test_crypto_import.py`, `tests/test_crypto_import_ui.py`, `tests/test_xtb_reconciliation.py`, `tests/test_xtb_import_ui.py`, `tests/test_transaction_saving.py`, `tests/test_quantity_corrections_ui.py`.

Decisions: `PRICED_BUY` takes the Sell's own price and rates (the issue's "day's price": the price the Sell was made at that day), so no price lookup is needed and its cost equals what the Sell brings for those units. A Sell naming its Lot (an Early Redemption of a Bond Series) is not corrected: the search stops there and the dialog only refuses it, as bonds are bought and redeemed in whole units. A gap above `LARGE_GAP` preselects no fix, so the import waits until one is chosen; without a correction it could not be applied anyway.
