# `domain/valuation.py`

`instrument_price(instrument, quote, rate_for)` decides the price a Position is valued at and its `PriceStatus`: the Manual Price while it is set (carrying the newest Quote to show beside it), else the newest Quote, else no price; no NBP Rate to convert it is its own status, and the rate is asked for only when there is a price. Its `Badge` (ręczna, nieaktualna when `stale`, bez źródła) shows beside the Position. A physical metal (`InstrumentDraft.metal_grams`, the metal weight of one unit, and `buyback_spread_percent`, migration 0015) has its prices (Manual Price and Quotes) as the spot of a troy ounce (`GRAMS_PER_TROY_OUNCE`): `unit_price` scales it to the unit less the spread (`unit_spot` without it), and `InstrumentPrice.spot` and `unit_spot_pln` keep the spot to show beside it. The Portfolio only displays it.
