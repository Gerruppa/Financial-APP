# Bond help window (#272, spec 3.4.1)

`domain/bond_guide.py` `guide_periods(series, purchase_day, cpi, reference)` lists every interest period of a bond of a Bond Series bought that day as a `GuidePeriod` (`number` from 1, `start`, `end`, `rate`, `source`): a capitalised type by year (TOS too, its one fixed rate each year), a coupon type by its coupon period (a year for COI, a month for ROR and DOR), the OTS as one period to maturity. The rate is the valuation's own, `domain/bond_valuation.py` `period_rate` (formerly `_rate`; `period_months` formerly `_period_months`), and `source` says where it comes from: `FROM_MF` "z pliku MF", `CORRECTED` "poprawione ręcznie" (a `RateCorrection`), the `RateLabel` of an estimate ("szacowana (CPI)", "prognoza (ostatnie CPI)", NBP alike) or `NO_DATA` "brak danych" (no rate: shown as "?").

`ui/bond_guide.py` `open_bond_guide(engine, series_name, purchase_day)` opens a movable dialog (`bond-guide`): the chosen Series' periods (`bond-guide-periods`, "Rok 1 · 12.10.2026–12.10.2027 · 5,35 % · z pliku MF", "Miesiąc n" for ROR and DOR, "Okres 1" for OTS; the held Series with its corrections from `load_bond_data`, else the MF terms; without a series or date a hint to type the purchase date), then static Polish help: `TYPE_HELP` (each `BondType`'s term and how it pays interest; a test checks every type is there), `ROLLOVER_HELP` (what a Rollover saves, which date and price) and `CHECKLIST` (what to compare with the broker's statement). It opens from the "+" dialog's bond mode (`transaction-bond-help`, shown with the bond fields: the chosen Series and the typed date) and the Rollover dialog (`rollover-help`: the Series the chosen type rolls into, from the maturity day). Tests: `tests/test_bond_guide_domain.py`, `tests/test_bond_guide_ui.py`.
