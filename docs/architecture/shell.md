# `ui/shell.py`

The NiceGUI window (left menu, header with the KAPITA logo `app-logo` (`APP_LOGO`), `APP_TITLE` and `financial_app.__version__`, the View Scope switcher, "+" transaction button `add-transaction`, hidden on Ustawienia and Pomoc (`WITHOUT_ADD_BUTTON`, L-28, #156)). Tabs are `ui.sub_pages` routes; `build_shell(engine)` hands the engine to the pages that need the database (`ui/settings.py` Ustawienia with `ui/accounts.py` Konta and `ui/instruments.py` Klasy aktywów and Instrumenty, `ui/transactions.py` Transakcje tab and the transaction dialog, opened empty by "+" or filled by clicking a listed Transaction to edit or delete it; the Account (`transaction-account`, filled light blue with a bold name, #118) comes before the type (`transaction-type`); a foreign-currency trade offers the payment source ("Płatność") on an Account holding the currency and the "Prowizja <broker> (przewalutowanie X%)" checkbox on an Account with an FX Conversion Fee, `ui/portfolio.py` read-only Portfolio from `todays_view`: an expandable card per Account with Cash Balance and Positions at the Manual Price, foreign ones converted at the NBP Rate; clicking a Position shows its Lots with both costs; foreign cash with its cost, value and the Account's FX result, left out of the Account's result when the Account excludes it; and the Account's net Dividends and its commissions and costs). After a save, the dialog's `on_saved` callback publishes `Change.TRANSACTIONS`.
