# `ui/shell.py`

The NiceGUI window in the window's Theme (`ui/theme.py`, #174): the left menu (`nav-menu`, a `ui.left_drawer` with `top_corner`, so it runs the full height) with the KAPITA logo `app-logo` (`APP_LOGO`), `APP_TITLE` and `financial_app.__version__` at its top (`app-version`, opening the changelog, see `changelog.md`, #200), the tab on screen lit (`k-active`) and Ustawienia and Pomoc below a separator, then the saying of the day `menu-saying` (`ui/sayings.py`, #178); the header (`header`) with the search field (`header-search`, `ui/header_search.py`, opening the Instrument Card, see `header-search.md`, #175), the "Pierwsze kroki n/3" chip while a first-run step is not done (`first-run-chip`, see `first-run.md`, #288), the status buttons, the refresh, the View Scope switcher and the User's avatar `user-menu` (`ui/user_menu.py`, #177, see `users.md`); "+" transaction button `add-transaction`, hidden on the Dashboard, Ustawienia and Pomoc (`WITHOUT_ADD_BUTTON`, L-28, #156)). Tabs are `ui.sub_pages` routes; `build_shell(engine)` hands the engine to the pages that need the database (`ui/settings.py` Ustawienia, its sections behind a row of tab buttons `settings-section-<key>` (Użytkownik, Konta, Limity i reguły, Klasy aktywów, Instrumenty, Import, Wygląd i widoczność, Wyczyść dane; only the chosen one is shown, the first unless `SettingsSection` names another, see `first-run.md`; Konta has "Pierwsze kroki" `first-run-open`), with `ui/accounts.py` Konta and `ui/instruments.py` Klasy aktywów and Instrumenty, `ui/transactions.py` Transakcje tab and `ui/transaction_dialog.py` the transaction dialog, opened empty by "+" or filled by clicking a listed Transaction to edit or delete it; the Account (`transaction-account`, filled in the Theme's highlighted field colour, light blue in Jasny with a bold name, #118) comes before the type (`transaction-type`); a foreign-currency trade offers the payment source ("Płatność") on an Account holding the currency and the "Prowizja <broker> (przewalutowanie X%)" checkbox on an Account with an FX Conversion Fee, `ui/portfolio.py` read-only Portfolio from `todays_view`, its Positions optionally grouped by "Grupuj" (#198, `instrument-types.md`): an expandable card per Account with Cash Balance and Positions at the Manual Price, foreign ones converted at the NBP Rate; clicking a Position shows its Lots with both costs; foreign cash with its cost, value and the Account's FX result, left out of the Account's result when the Account excludes it; and the Account's net Dividends and its commissions and costs). After a save, the dialog's `on_saved` callback publishes `Change.TRANSACTIONS`.

The header's eye button `discreet-toggle` blurs the amounts (see `discreet.md`). The header's "Wesprzyj" button (`support-button`, `build_support_button`, left of the avatar, hidden in the narrowest window, icon only below 1440 px) opens `SUPPORT_URL` (buycoffee.to, in `ui/user_menu.py`) with `webbrowser.open`, in the system browser, because in the native window a link would replace the app.

Sign-out (#195, `users.md`): `build_shell(on_switch_user=...)` passes the callback to the avatar's menu, `build_shell(user_section=...)` the Użytkownik card to Ustawienia (#196), and `stop_background_work(engine)` cancels that engine's shared refresh and History (`ui/history.py` `forget_history`).

The Portfolio tab (`ui/portfolio.py`) has an "Eksportuj XLSX" control (`portfolio-export`, `portfolio-export-year`, #241) saving the Portfolio at the end of a chosen year; see `export.md`.

With `build_shell(offer_first_run=True)` (`app.py`) a database with no Account opens the first-run window at start (`ui/first_run.py`, `first-run.md`, #270).
