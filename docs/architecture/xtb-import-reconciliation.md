# XTB import and Reconciliation (L-17, spec 7)

`domain/xtb_import.py` reads the "Cash Operations" sheet of xStation's export (`parse_statement` → `XtbStatement`, the account number from its first row; `warsaw_day` dates UTC times without tzdata) into `XtbEntry`s (`xtb_entries`: Buys and Sells from the comment, parts of one order merged, interest with its month's tax, transfers paired across the statements imported together into a `TRANSFER`, else a Deposit or Withdrawal, the rest `UNSUPPORTED`; `external_id` "xtb-" + the lowest cash row id, a transfer's both legs; `unresolved` leaves only the leg not imported before), `xtb_draft` (Origin `BROKER`; a foreign trade a `BrokerConversion` of the PLN XTB charged), `yahoo_symbol` and `match_instrument`. `domain/reconciliation.py` `reconcile` pairs broker drafts with the entered Transactions (Account, type, Instrument, quantity, target, ±`DATE_WINDOW` days, amount within 1% without an Instrument) and the `incoming` Cash Transfers into its Accounts (paired with a broker Deposit or Withdrawal as `linked`, always kept) into `Pair`s (`same`: date, amount, price, commission, tax), broker-only and entered-only; `Choice` BROKER (default) or MINE, kept by `item_key`. `persistence/reconciliation.py` `apply_reconciliation` applies `AddBroker`, `TakeBroker`, `KeepMine`, `DeleteMine`, `SkipBroker` all or none and replays the touched Positions; `handled_broker_ids`, `unreconciled_transactions` (within the statement's days, only `RECONCILED_TYPES`, no foreign Dividends) and `incoming_transfers` (both PLN only: a foreign Cash Transfer, #125, is none of the statement's) read `transactions.broker_id`, `reconciled_on` and the `skipped_broker_rows` table (migration 0026). `sources/xtb.py` (`read_statements` of a file or folder, `preview_xtb`, `apply_xtb`) refuses a non-PLN XTB account, matches accounts (the one IKE/IKZE Account suggested) and tickers, remembered in `app_settings` under `persistence/settings.py` `XTB_ACCOUNT`/`XTB_TICKER` (cleared by `clear_portfolio`). `ui/xtb_import.py` `XtbImportSection` is its card in Ustawienia, whose `xtb-open` opens a `Wizard` (prefix `xtb`, `wizard.md`, #271) of eight `STEPS`: 1 `INSTRUCTIONS` (which statements to export), 2 `xtb-path`, `xtb-load` (`load` jumps to step 3), `xtb-loaded`, 3 `xtb-account-<number>`, `xtb-instrument-<ticker>` whose "+ Nowy instrument…" opens `InstrumentsSection.open_dialog(None, query=, on_saved=)`, `xtb-problem` (also in step 8; "Dalej" held until all are matched), 4 `xtb-summary`, `xtb-all-broker`/`xtb-all-mine`, the differing pairs (`xtb-no-different` when none), the linked Cash Transfers and `xtb-same`, 5 XTB-only (`xtb-no-broker-only`, `xtb-unsupported`), 6 entered-only (`xtb-no-mine-only`), each item a `xtb-item` with toggle `xtb-choice-<key>`, 7 `xtb-corrections-<n>` and the covering Deposits (`xtb-deposits-<account id>`, `xtb-no-deposits`), 8 `xtb-plan`, `xtb-deposits-summary`, `xtb-corrections-summary`, `xtb-corrections-missing`, `xtb-apply`, `xtb-done` and `HINTS` (`xtb-hints`). Covering Deposits (#271): `preview_xtb` fills `XtbPreview.deposits` with `covering_deposits` of each imported Account on `after_actions` of the chosen actions with their corrections; `actions()` adds those of the Accounts not in `XtbDecisions.no_deposits` (`kept_deposits`), so `apply_xtb` saves them with the rest. `tests/test_xtb_parity.py` checks the user's exports in `reference/` (units against "Open Positions", cash against the total); tests build statements with `tests/xtb_rows.py`.

Quantity corrections (#278, `quantity-corrections.md`): `XtbPreview.shortfalls` are the Sells the chosen Reconciliation (`chosen_actions`, simulated by `after_actions`) leaves uncovered, each with its fix (`XtbDecisions.corrections`); `actions()` adds the fixes, `blocked` and `apply_xtb` wait for every one, and the window lists them in step 7 (`xtb-corrections-<n>`).
