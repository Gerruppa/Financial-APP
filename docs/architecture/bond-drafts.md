# Maturity and Rollover Drafts (L-6, spec 3.4.1)

`domain/bond_drafts.py` `maturity_drafts(positions, instruments, accounts, bonds, day, account_id)` gives a `MaturityDraft` per Lot of a Bond Series held on or after its `maturity` (earliest first), its `redemption` the `EarlyRedemption` of all its bonds that day (no fee, the Tax Regime's tax; `None` for a Series not valued, entered by hand). `transaction()` is the Sell naming the Lot on the maturity day, `rollover_purchase(instrument_id, quantity, price)` the Buy of the new Series that day; `rollover_series(type)` names it (`ROLLOVER_TYPES`, no family bonds; at most the Lot's bonds). `persistence/bond_drafts.py`: `pending_drafts(engine, day, account_id)`, `confirm_draft`, `confirm_rollover` (both Transactions or neither, the new Series removed again on failure) and `rollover_price` (MF's, else 99.90). `ui/bond_drafts.py`: `drafts_list` at the top of the Dochód stały tab, with the Term Deposits' Drafts (#160) (`bond-drafts`, rows `bond-draft`, `confirm-redemption`, `rollover` opening `rollover-dialog` with `rollover-type`, `rollover-quantity`, `save-rollover`), following the View Scope; `drafts_count` in the header (`drafts-count`, "Do zatwierdzenia: n" over every Account, opening the tab), redrawn after a save, a price or bond data refresh. Drafts are never stored: a saved redemption closes the Lot. Coupons (L-8): `coupon_drafts(..., entered)` gives a `CouponDraft` per coupon paid by the day of a held COI, ROR or DOR Lot, skipping as many of the oldest as `entered` counts for the Lot's `opened_by` (`coupons_entered`: the interest Dividends naming it, whatever their day); its `transaction()` is an interest Dividend (no Instrument) with `lot_id` naming the Lot (`TransactionDraft` allows `lot_id` on a Sell or an interest Dividend; `dividend(..., lot_id=)`), the tax per bond rounded half up. `bond_drafts` merges both kinds (`BondDraft`) by `due`; `persistence/bond_drafts.py` `pending_drafts` returns them and `confirm_draft` saves either; `delete_transaction` blocks deleting a Buy that interest names; `ui/bond_drafts.py` draws coupon rows (`confirm-coupon`), counted in the header, and the transaction dialog keeps an edited interest Dividend's `lot_id`. `persistence/bond_series.py` `drop_if_unused` removes a Series created for a save that failed.
