# Header search and Instrument Card (#175)

`ui/header_search.py` `HeaderSearch(engine, search, open_card)` is the header's search field (`header-search`, built first in the header by `ui/shell.py`) with its list below it (`header-search-results`): "Twoje instrumenty" lists at once, at each keystroke, up to `OWN_LIMIT` (5) of the user's Instruments whose name, ISIN or a Source Symbol contains the query, ignoring case and Polish letters (`domain/header_search.py` `matching_instruments`, through `domain/formatting.py` `folded`; rows `own-result`); "Znalezione" asks the shell's `InstrumentSearch` (Yahoo and analizy.pl through `CombinedSearch`) once `MIN_QUERY` (3) characters are typed and `PAUSE` (0,5 s) passed, or on Enter, and lists up to `FOUND_LIMIT` (8) rows (`found-result`), one per fund or company (`group_results` gives a `SearchResult` of `FoundListing`s per ISIN, or per name when the search gives none, with its listings' symbols, Asset Classes and sources as badges); a failed service is noted (`header-search-message`, "<źródło> nie odpowiada"); results of a query typed over meanwhile are dropped. Arrows move the highlighted row (`k-highlight`); Enter opens it, a Search Result once this query's results are in (else it asks the services), an own Instrument at once; Esc clears the field; Ctrl+K anywhere or "/" outside a field focuses it (`_SHORTCUT_SCRIPT`, in the browser, so that the key is not typed into it). The card opens in the field's outer element (`HeaderSearch.box`), not in its row: a NiceGUI dialog is deleted once the element it was opened in is collected, and the results list redraws when the search answers. A Search Result listed in Ustawienia > Instrumenty once added shows there at once, as the Instrument dialog's save redraws the list on screen.

`ui/instrument_card.py` `open_instrument_card(CardContext, subject)` builds an `InstrumentCard` and opens the Instrument Card (`instrument-card`, a `ui.dialog` of 1100 px, at most 95vw) on a `SearchResult` (its first listing; several listings give a choice, `card-listing`) or on one of the user's Instruments (shown as a listing of its own Source Symbols): the name, ISIN with a copy button (`card-isin`), the symbols, Asset Class and source badges, and market, currency, domicile and Distribution Policy (`card-details`; a Search Result's from `fill_empty`, the domicile by the ISIN's country, the policy by Acc/Dist in its name). A listing no Instrument holds (`domain/header_search.py` `owned_instrument`: none has any of its Source Symbols; the same ISIN on another exchange or in another currency is another Instrument) has "Dodaj jako instrument" (`card-add`), which opens `ui/instruments.py` `InstrumentsSection.open_dialog(None, prefill=listing)`, its empty fields filled as if the listing were chosen among its search results; once saved the card says "Dodano instrument <nazwa>" and shows "Masz już ten instrument" (`card-owned`) with "Edytuj" (`card-edit`, the Instrument dialog), as for an Instrument already held. The user's Instrument also shows "Twoja pozycja" (`card-position`): its quantity, value and result on each Account from `todays_view`, whatever the View Scope.

The price part loads in the background (`card-loading`): `sources/instrument_card.py` `CardHistory.closes(listing, last)` fetches the listing's whole history (`FIRST_DAY`) live from its Yahoo symbol, else its analizy.pl Fund Code; nothing is saved, so a Search Result leaves no trace and needs no migration; without either symbol the card of a held Instrument shows its saved Quotes (`persistence/history.py` `quotes_between`, e.g. a share priced from Bossa only), else there is none (`card-no-history`), and a source error is shown (`card-history-error`). `domain/instrument_card.py` `price_summary` gives the price panel (`card-price`: the last close, its change from the close before and since the last close of the year before, the lowest and highest close since the same day a year before, `card-low-12m` and `card-high-12m`), `chart_window(quotes, period)` the chart's closes for each `CardPeriod` (YTD, 1M … 5L, Max; `card-periods`; its own type rather than `domain/periods.py` `PeriodPreset`, which has no 6M or Max and counts back from today, not the last close) from the close its return counts from, drawn by `ui/charts.py` `price_chart` (`chart-price`) in the quote currency, its tooltip from the shared `_formatter(unit, decimals)`, and `returns` the returns table (`card-returns`): each of the last `RETURN_YEARS` (5) calendar years and each of `RETURN_PERIODS` (1M, 3M, 6M, 1R, 3L, headed by `CardPeriod.name_pl`), "—" where the history starts later, from closes only, so a distributing fund's are noted as without its dividends. There is no description, TER, AuM or documents: the sources give none (scraping atlasetf.pl or justETF is ruled out; #188 looks at Yahoo `quoteSummary`). `build_shell(..., card_history=...)` takes a test history. Tests: `tests/test_header_search_domain.py`, `tests/test_instrument_card_domain.py`, `tests/test_instrument_card_history.py` (saved Yahoo and analizy.pl responses) and `tests/test_header_search_ui.py`.
