# Header search and Instrument Card (#175)

`ui/header_search.py` `HeaderSearch(engine, search, open_card)` is the header's search field (`header-search`, built first in the header by `ui/shell.py`) with its list below it (`header-search-results`): "Twoje instrumenty" lists at once, at each keystroke, up to `OWN_LIMIT` (5) of the user's Instruments whose name, ISIN or a Source Symbol contains the query, ignoring case and Polish letters (`domain/header_search.py` `matching_instruments`, through `domain/formatting.py` `folded`; rows `own-result`); "Znalezione" asks the shell's `InstrumentSearch` (Yahoo and analizy.pl through `CombinedSearch`) once `MIN_QUERY` (3) characters are typed and `PAUSE` (0,5 s) passed, or on Enter, and lists up to `FOUND_LIMIT` (8) rows (`found-result`), one per fund or company (`group_results` gives a `SearchResult` of `FoundListing`s per ISIN, or per name when the search gives none, with its listings' symbols, Asset Classes and sources as badges); a failed service is noted (`header-search-message`, "<źródło> nie odpowiada"); results of a query typed over meanwhile are dropped. Arrows move the highlighted row (`k-highlight`); Enter opens it, a Search Result once this query's results are in (else it asks the services), an own Instrument at once; Esc clears the field; Ctrl+K anywhere or "/" outside a field focuses it (`_SHORTCUT_SCRIPT`, in the browser, so that the key is not typed into it). The card opens in the field's outer element (`HeaderSearch.box`), not in its row: a NiceGUI dialog is deleted once the element it was opened in is collected, and the results list redraws when the search answers. A Search Result listed in Ustawienia > Instrumenty once added shows there at once, as the Instrument dialog's save redraws the list on screen.

Command palette: the same field finds commands. `domain/commands.py` `Command(key, label, keywords)`, `matching_commands(commands, query)` (every word of the query must start a word of the label or a keyword, ignoring case and Polish letters; from `COMMAND_MIN_QUERY` (2) characters, at most `COMMAND_LIMIT` (6)) and `in_command_mode`: a query starting with `COMMAND_PREFIX` (">") lists commands only, all of them for a bare ">" and without a limit, and then neither the own Instruments, the bond types nor the search services are asked. `ui/header_search.py` `PaletteCommand(command, icon, run)` and `HeaderSearch(..., commands)`: a "Polecenia" section (rows `command-result`) after the bond types and before "Znalezione" (so an Instrument named like a command still comes first on Enter); Enter or a click closes the list, clears the field and runs the command inside `HeaderSearch.box` (a dialog it opens must outlive the redrawn list); "Brak pasujących poleceń." after ">" with no match; the panel scrolls (`max-h-[75vh]`). `ui/palette.py` `palette_commands(tabs, go_to=, add_transaction=, switch_theme=, switch_discreet=, refresh_prices=, switch_user=)` gives "Przejdź do: <tab>" for every tab (`TAB_KEYWORDS` adds words such as "obligacje" for Dochód stały), "Dodaj transakcję", "Przełącz motyw (Jasny/Golden)", "Tryb dyskrecji: ukryj lub pokaż kwoty" (`ui/discreet.py` `switch_discreet`, shared with the eye button), "Odśwież wyceny" and, when the window has a login screen, "Przełącz użytkownika"; `ui/shell.py` wires them. Tests: `tests/test_commands_domain.py`, `tests/test_command_palette_ui.py`.

`ui/instrument_card.py` `open_instrument_card(CardContext, subject)` builds an `InstrumentCard` and opens the Instrument Card (`instrument-card`, a `ui.dialog` of 1100 px, at most 95vw) on a `SearchResult` (its first listing; several listings give a choice, `card-listing`) or on one of the user's Instruments (shown as a listing of its own Source Symbols): the name, ISIN with a copy button (`card-isin`), the symbols, Asset Class and source badges, and market, currency, domicile and Distribution Policy (`card-details`; a Search Result's from `fill_empty`, the domicile by the ISIN's country, the policy by Acc/Dist in its name). A listing no Instrument holds (`domain/header_search.py` `owned_instrument`: none has any of its Source Symbols; the same ISIN on another exchange or in another currency is another Instrument) has "Dodaj jako instrument" (`card-add`), which opens `ui/instruments.py` `InstrumentsSection.open_dialog(None, prefill=listing)`, its empty fields filled as if the listing were chosen among its search results; once saved the card says "Dodano instrument <nazwa>" and shows "Masz już ten instrument" (`card-owned`) with "Edytuj" (`card-edit`, the Instrument dialog), as for an Instrument already held. The user's Instrument also shows "Twoja pozycja" (`card-position`): its quantity, value and result on each Account from `todays_view`, whatever the View Scope.

The price part loads in the background (`card-loading`): `sources/instrument_card.py` `CardHistory.closes(listing, last)` fetches the listing's whole history (`FIRST_DAY`) live from its Yahoo symbol, else its analizy.pl Fund Code; nothing is saved, so a Search Result leaves no trace and needs no migration; without either symbol the card of a held Instrument shows its saved Quotes (`persistence/history.py` `quotes_between`, e.g. a share priced from Bossa only), else there is none (`card-no-history`), and a source error is shown (`card-history-error`). `domain/instrument_card.py` `price_summary` gives the price panel (`card-price`: the last close, its change from the close before and since the last close of the year before, the lowest and highest close since the same day a year before, `card-low-12m` and `card-high-12m`), `chart_window(quotes, period)` the chart's closes for each `CardPeriod` (YTD, 1M … 5L, Max; `card-periods`; its own type rather than `domain/periods.py` `PeriodPreset`, which has no 6M or Max and counts back from today, not the last close) from the close its return counts from, drawn by `ui/charts.py` `price_chart` (`chart-price`) in the quote currency, its tooltip from the shared `_formatter(unit, decimals)`, and `returns` the returns table (`card-returns`): each of the last `RETURN_YEARS` (5) calendar years and each of `RETURN_PERIODS` (1M, 3M, 6M, 1R, 3L, headed by `CardPeriod.label`), "—" where the history starts later, from closes only, so a distributing fund's are noted as without its dividends. The fund profile (#188) loads in the background beside it and never delays the card (`InstrumentCard.profile`, `card-profile`): `sources/fund_profile.py` `YahooFundProfiles(transport).profile(symbol)` asks Yahoo `quoteSummary` (summaryDetail, fundProfile, defaultKeyStatistics) with a cookie from fc.yahoo.com and a crumb from `/v1/test/getcrumb`, both kept in memory for the session and fetched once more after a 401, each symbol asked once per session; any failure, a missing symbol or a profile with nothing in it gives `None`, and the section is then simply absent (no note). `domain/fund_profile.py` `FundProfile` holds the TER (a percent, from `annualReportExpenseRatio`), the fund size (`summaryDetail.totalAssets`, kept only with `summaryDetail.currency`; `format_fund_size` writes "88,97 mld USD") and the issuer (`fundProfile.family`). Only a listing with a Yahoo symbol that is a fund or ETF (the `fund` flag, or an Instrument of type ETF or Fundusz) and not a `.WA` one is asked. The card shows "TER" (`card-ter`), "Wielkość funduszu" (`card-fund-size`) and "Emitent" (`card-issuer`), those that came, marked "wg Yahoo"; an Instrument's own TER wins and Yahoo's shows only when it has none, as "(Yahoo)", and is never saved silently. "Dodaj jako instrument" passes the TER on in `FoundListing.ter_percent`, which `fill_empty` and the Instrument dialog take while the TER field is blank. Nothing is stored. `build_shell(..., fund_profiles=...)` takes a test source; tests: `tests/test_fund_profile.py` (saved responses in `tests/fixtures/yahoo/summary/`) and `tests/test_header_search_ui.py`. Polish funds, PPK and GPW ETFs (nothing in Yahoo) get the TFI, the group ("Grupa analizy.pl", `card-group`) and the benchmark (`card-benchmark`) instead, marked "wg analizy.pl" (#204): `ui/instrument_card.py` `_profile_request` picks the source (Yahoo by its symbol; for a `.WA` listing or one without a Yahoo symbol analizy.pl by its Fund Code, else by the ticker, from the Yahoo symbol without `.WA` or the Bossa one), and `sources/analizy.py` `AnalizyFundProfiles(fetch)` reads `firmaZarz`, `nazwaGrupy` and `searchBenchmark` from the search JSON ("fundusz nie posiada benchmarku" counts as none); a fund is the hit with exactly its code, an ETF needs exactly one hit of kind ETF, otherwise nothing; each query is asked once per session, any failure gives no section, nothing is stored and no TER comes (analizy.pl has it only in HTML, so it stays manual). `build_shell(..., polish_profiles=...)` takes a test source; tests: `tests/test_analizy_profile.py` (`tests/fixtures/analizy/search/`). The largest holdings (#203, `card-holdings`, one `card-holding` per row, "Nazwa: 5,79 %", headed "Największe składniki (wg <źródło>[, stan na <dzień>])") are `FundProfile.holdings` (`Holding(name, percent)`, largest first) and `holdings_date`: Yahoo's `topHoldings` module (`holdingName`, `holdingPercent.raw` as a fraction; the module rides in the same `quoteSummary` request) for non-Warsaw listings, and for a Polish fund or PPK `AnalizyFundProfiles.fund_profile` also asks `/api/assets/top10/<Fund Code>` (`struct` of `name` and `value` fractions, `date` the end of a quarter); a failed or `{"success": false}` answer only leaves the holdings out, and a GPW ETF is not asked (analizy.pl has none). A profile with holdings only draws them without the `card-profile` row. The geographic structure (`/api/assets/geogr/`) is not shown. Fixtures: `tests/fixtures/analizy/top10/`. There is no description or documents: the sources give none (scraping atlasetf.pl or justETF is ruled out). `build_shell(..., card_history=...)` takes a test history. Tests: `tests/test_header_search_domain.py`, `tests/test_instrument_card_domain.py`, `tests/test_instrument_card_history.py` (saved Yahoo and analizy.pl responses) and `tests/test_header_search_ui.py`. The card shows the Instrument Type badge `card-instrument-type` (#198). The chart's currency (#187): `card-currency` in the price panel offers `domain/instrument_card.py` `chart_currencies` (the quote currency, then PLN, EUR, USD); a choice makes `InstrumentCard._show` convert every close with `convert_quotes` through PLN at the NBP Rate in force on its day (the last published on or before it; a close with no rate yet is dropped), rates from `NbpRates.history` (cached, fetched in the background from `LOOKBACK` before the first close), so the price panel, chart and returns are all in that currency, noted by `card-converted`; if the rates cannot be fetched the card says so and stays in the quote currency. Nothing is stored; the choice resets with the listing.

Treasury bond types (#185): `domain/header_search.py` `matching_bond_types(query)` lists at once, at each keystroke from `BOND_MIN_QUERY` (2) characters, the `BondType`s whose code or a term in `BOND_TYPE_TERMS` ("10-letnie", "czteroletnie", "rodzinne") starts with the query, ignoring case and Polish letters (from the start, so "2-letnie" is not the 12-year ROD); the list "Obligacje skarbowe" (rows `bond-result`) sits between "Twoje instrumenty" and "Znalezione", and Enter opens a highlighted bond row at once. The row opens `ui/bond_type_card.py` `open_bond_type_card` (`bond-type-card`, not the Instrument Card; `open_instrument_card` dispatches on a `BondType`): the current offer (`bond-offer`: `domain/bonds.py` `current_offer`, the `mf_bond_series` Series of the type on sale today, with its sale period, `bond-term`, `bond-first-rate` and `bond-rule` from `RATE_RULES` plus the margin, or a note when the type is not sold now), the user's Bond Series of the type with the bonds held and their value (`bond-own-series`, `bond-own-row`) and "Kup" (`bond-buy`), which opens `open_transaction_dialog(..., start_with=FormPrefill(..., bond_type=...))`: a Buy today in the bond mode of that type, on the first Bonds Account (else the first Account); `CardContext.on_bought` is the shell's `transactions_changed`. No chart and no "Dodaj jako instrument": the Bond Series is created on its first purchase, so no migration. Tests: `tests/test_header_search_domain.py`, `tests/test_header_search_ui.py`.
