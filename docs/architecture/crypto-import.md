# Crypto exchange import (Binance, OKX; spec 7)

`domain/crypto_import.py` reads the CSV text of an export: `detect_exchange` (by header), `parse_binance` (the "Transaction History" ledger: "Buy Crypto With Fiat" and "Binance Convert" by the sign of Change, a "Transaction Buy" merged with its "Transaction Fee" of the same second and coin, so the quantity is net of the fee; Spot/Funding moves ignored, the rest `Unsupported`, e.g. a Withdraw) and `parse_okx_orders` (the "Order History": completed Spot Buy/Sell only; a Buy's fee in the coin bought is taken out of the quantity, the price per unit received rising so the cost stays; a Sell's fee in the quote coin is `fee`). `CryptoTrade.external_id` is "binance-<time>-<operation>-<coin>-<n>" or "okx-<order id>". `crypto_draft(trade, CryptoContext)` makes the broker-origin `buy_or_sell` draft: a USD/USDC/USDT price needs a USD Instrument, valued at the NBP USD rate, the fee a commission in PLN; Binance's missing price is the day's market price of the context.

`sources/crypto.py`: `read_crypto` (a file, or the CSV files of a folder of one exchange; OKX's "Trade Details" give only the exact quantity: the Order History rounds Filled Amount to 4 places, so `parse_okx_fills` sums each fill's Trading Volume / Filled Price per order, rounded to 8 places (`UNIT`), and `parse_exchange` hands it to `parse_okx_orders`; the price and fee still come from the order, and an order without fills keeps the rounded quantity with an `Unsupported` warning), `yahoo_prices` (`PriceLookup`: Yahoo's daily close, else the last one in the 7 days before), `match_coin` (Yahoo symbol "BTC-USD" or the name), `preview_crypto` and `apply_crypto` (all or none through `apply_reconciliation` with `AddBroker`; trades already in `handled_broker_ids` are not offered again; the Account per exchange and the Instrument per coin remembered in `app_settings` under `CRYPTO_ACCOUNT`/`CRYPTO_COIN`, cleared with the XTB ones). There is no Reconciliation with entered Transactions, only additions.

`ui/crypto_import.py` `CryptoImportSection` is the card beside the XTB one in Ustawienia > Import (`crypto-path`, `crypto-load`, `crypto-account`, `crypto-instrument-<SYMBOL>`, `crypto-summary`, `crypto-item`, `crypto-unsupported`, `crypto-apply`).

A Binance "Withdraw" is a `CryptoTransfer` (`CryptoRead.transfers`): of the rows at the same second the largest is the amount moved, the smaller the network fee, listed as `Unsupported` (a loss to enter by hand). `transfer_draft` makes a broker-origin Security Transfer from the exchange's Account to the wallet Account, which keeps the Lots' dates and costs. The wallet Account is chosen in the card (`crypto-wallet`, shown only when the file has withdrawals not imported before), remembered per exchange under `CRYPTO_WALLET`; without it `CryptoPreview.needs_wallet` blocks applying. A transfer larger than what the Account holds fails the whole apply (nothing saved).

Decisions: Binance prices are an approximation (the day's Yahoo close, in the Instrument's currency), not what was paid; the network fee of a withdrawal is not imported.
