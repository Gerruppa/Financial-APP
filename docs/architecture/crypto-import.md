# Crypto exchange import (Binance, OKX, Trezor; spec 7)

`domain/crypto_import.py` reads the CSV text of an export: `detect_exchange` (by header), `parse_binance` (the "Transaction History" ledger: "Buy Crypto With Fiat" and "Binance Convert" by the sign of Change, a "Transaction Buy" merged with its "Transaction Fee" of the same second and coin, so the quantity is net of the fee; Spot/Funding moves ignored, the rest `Unsupported`, e.g. a Withdraw) and `parse_okx_orders` (the "Order History": completed Spot Buy/Sell only; a Buy's fee in the coin bought is taken out of the quantity, the price per unit received rising so the cost stays; a Sell's fee in the quote coin is `fee`). `CryptoTrade.external_id` is "binance-<time>-<operation>-<coin>-<n>" or "okx-<order id>". `crypto_draft(trade, CryptoContext)` makes the broker-origin `buy_or_sell` draft: a USD/USDC/USDT price needs a USD Instrument, valued at the NBP USD rate, the fee a commission in PLN; Binance's missing price is the day's market price of the context.

`sources/crypto.py`: `read_crypto` (a file, or the CSV files of a folder and its subfolders, any sources: one `CryptoFile` per source, the exchanges before the wallet, a row in two files, e.g. a copy, counted once by its external id; OKX's "Trade Details" give only the exact quantity: the Order History rounds Filled Amount to 4 places, so `parse_okx_fills` sums each fill's Trading Volume / Filled Price per order, rounded to 8 places (`UNIT`), and `parse_exchange` hands it to `parse_okx_orders`; the price and fee still come from the order, and an order without fills keeps the rounded quantity with an `Unsupported` warning), `yahoo_prices` (`PriceLookup`: Yahoo's daily close, else the last one in the 7 days before), `match_coin` (Yahoo symbol "BTC-USD" or the name), `preview_crypto` (a `CryptoPreview` per file, in order) and `apply_crypto` (every source at once, all or none through `apply_reconciliation`; trades already in `handled_broker_ids` are not offered again; `CryptoDecisions` holds the Account and wallet per source and the Instrument per coin, remembered in `app_settings` under `CRYPTO_ACCOUNT`/`CRYPTO_WALLET` + source and `CRYPTO_COIN`, cleared with the XTB ones). There is no Reconciliation with entered Transactions, only additions and the transfer pairs below.

`ui/crypto_import.py` `CryptoImportSection` is the card beside the XTB one in Ustawienia > Import, with a section per source (`crypto-path`, `crypto-load`, `crypto-source-<source>`, `crypto-account` and `crypto-account-<source>`, `crypto-wallet` and `crypto-wallet-<source>`, `crypto-instrument-<SYMBOL>` once for all sources, `crypto-summary` per source prefixed by its name, `crypto-item`, `crypto-unsupported`, one `crypto-apply`).

A Binance "Withdraw" is a `CryptoTransfer` (`CryptoRead.transfers`): of the rows at the same second the largest is the amount moved, the smaller the network fee, a `CryptoTrade` Sell with `fee_is_value` (id "binance-<time>-fee-<coin>-<n>"): `crypto_draft` sells the coins at the day's market price with a commission of their whole value less a grosz (a Sell must bring something), so the exchange's Account does not keep coins that are gone and the loss is in the result. `transfer_draft` makes a broker-origin Security Transfer from the exchange's Account to the wallet Account, which keeps the Lots' dates and costs. The wallet Account is chosen in the card (`crypto-wallet`, shown only when the file has withdrawals not imported before), remembered per exchange under `CRYPTO_WALLET`; without it `CryptoPreview.needs_wallet` blocks applying. A transfer larger than what the Account holds fails the whole apply (nothing saved).

OKX's "Funding History" (`parse_okx_funding`, detected by its `id`/`Type`/`Amount-USD` header, merged by `parse_exchange` with the orders): a "Buy" is a `CryptoTrade` in USD priced as `Amount-USD` / amount, a "Deposit" an `incoming` `CryptoTransfer` (`transfer_draft` then moves the Lots from the wallet Account to the exchange's), a "Withdrawal" an outgoing one, id "okx-funding-<id>". Quantities are rounded down to 8 places so a move never exceeds the buy. Stablecoin deposits and withdrawals (cash) and "To/From unified trading account" are ignored, any other type is `Unsupported`. Its times are shifted to UTC by the `Time Zone:UTC+8` of its first line; the Order History has no zone and is taken as UTC (the 2026 deposits come just before the sales they fund).

A Trezor Suite CSV (`TREZOR`, detected by its `Timestamp`/`Transaction ID`/`Amount unit` header; `parse_trezor`) is a wallet's, not an exchange's: its Account is the wallet. A RECV is an `incoming` `CryptoTransfer`, a SENT an outgoing one whose network fee (paid by the wallet) is a `fee_is_value` Sell as Binance's, a SELF only that fee; any other type (FAILED, ...) is `Unsupported`. Ids are "trezor-<transaction id>" ("-2" for a transaction's second row, "-fee" for the fee), days the UTC ones of the Timestamp. `CryptoFile.is_wallet` marks the Trezor's file; its Account defaults to the wallet the exchanges' withdrawals go to (chosen in the same import or remembered under `CRYPTO_WALLET`) when they all chose one.

Transfer pairs: a transfer between an exchange and the wallet is in both exports, so whichever is read second pairs with the first's Security Transfer instead of adding one. `pair_transfers` pairs a transfer into (receipt, deposit) or out of (send, withdrawal) the source's Account with a known one of the coin's Instrument, from or to the exchange's wallet (any other Account for the wallet's own export), within `PAIR_DAYS` (7) and `PAIR_TOLERANCE` (1 %) of the quantity, closest date first; a known one with the same source's id prefix is that source's own record, never a pair. The known ones are `persistence/reconciliation.py` `unpaired_security_transfers` (saved, no `broker_id` yet; not those an earlier source of the same import paired) and the drafts the sources before it add in the same import (so the wallet, read last, pairs with the exchanges' transfers of the same folder). A pair with a saved one is `KeepMine` (its `broker_id` becomes the pairing id), with a draft of the same import `AddBroker(draft, broker_id)`. Each wallet transfer left (`CryptoPreview.unpaired`) gets its other Account (`CryptoDecisions.counterparts`, a `transfer_draft` from or to it; the wallet's own Account counts as not chosen), is skipped (`skipped`, `SkipBroker`; the fee of a skipped SENT is still imported, as the coins left the wallet) or by default left for later (`CryptoPreview.later`: neither saved nor blocking, offered again by the next import, when the exchange's export that explains it may be in). In the card: `crypto-counterpart-<external id>` per unpaired transfer ("Później", "Pomiń" or an Account), `crypto-paired`; a wallet has no wallet select.

Decisions: Binance prices are an approximation (the day's Yahoo close, in the Instrument's currency), not what was paid; the network fee of a withdrawal is not imported.
