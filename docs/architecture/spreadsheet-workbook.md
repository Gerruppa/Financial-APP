# `sources/spreadsheet.py`

Opens the workbook (openpyxl), `preview_import` (matches Konta and Instruments, the user's `Decisions` resolve the rest, nothing saved) and `save_import` (creates the new Instruments and saves every row or none). `ui/import_sheet.py` is its section in Ustawienia; an unmatched Konto can create an Account through `ui/accounts.py` `open_account_dialog` (#63), prefilled by `cash_currencies_of`. Transactions keep their Origin (`domain/transactions.py` `Origin`, migration 0011 adds `origin` and `external_id`). An Instrument the import creates gets its Instrument Type from `infer_instrument_type` (#198).
