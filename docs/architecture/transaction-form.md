# Transaction form (#111)

`domain/transaction_form.py` `TransactionForm(FormContext)` holds the transaction dialog's rules without widgets. `FormContext` is every Account and Instrument, the Transactions in entry order, the Bond Series held and in the MF file, the `BondData`, the NBP Rate lookup (`rates`, `NbpRates.before` in the app) and the edited Transaction. Given a `FormState` (the choices and the typed `FormTexts`; `FormTexts.of(transaction)` gives an edit's exact texts) it gives `initial_state(today)`, `layout(state)` (`FormLayout`: each field's `Shown` visibility, label and hint and each select's `Choice` options and value, a choice no longer offered falling back to its default: the target Account, the Cash Currency paid from or into (`paid_from`), FX Conversion Fee checkbox, the currency of a Deposit, Withdrawal or Cost (`cash_currency`, marker `transaction-cash-currency`, a foreign Deposit or Withdrawal taking `fx_rate` too, #119), the interest currency, the Currency Exchange's currency and direction, the Types the Account can take (`kind`, from `kinds(state)`: an Account without PLN, #124, has no Currency Exchange (`PLN_ONLY_KINDS`; its Cash Transfers are in its own currencies, #125), a Cash Transfer, in PLN or a currency of the source (`cash_currency`, #125, at the NBP Rate), goes only to an Account holding that currency, and its cash amounts, interest and trades are in its own currencies, a trade in another refused; the commission's label and `commission_currency` follow), the bond mode's Series list and text, the Early Redemption's Lot), `draft(state, series_id)` (the `TransactionDraft`, or `FormError` naming the field that does not read; an edit reuses its own NBP Rate while the currency and date stay), `series_to_buy`, `bond_price`, `lot_quantity` and `redemption` (the Early Redemption prefill from `open_lots`, ADR-0007), `net_text`, `with_exchange` and `proposed_quantity` (#120: a Buy's `FormTexts.spend`, "Kwota do wydania" in the currency it is paid from, not saved, less the commission when it is in the same cash, at 1, the user's rate or the NBP Rate raised by the FX Conversion Fee when the broker converts, ÷ price, rounded down to the Instrument's `quantity_decimals`; the dialog's `transaction-spend` fills the quantity with it until the user types one). `ui/transaction_dialog.py` `open_transaction_dialog` only builds the widgets (same markers; the payment fields from `transaction-paid-from` to `transaction-auto-exchange` sit outside the Instrument fields, as a foreign Deposit takes a rate), reads them into a `FormState` and applies the layout (again when a value it set changes the state), keeping only what is the dialog's own: the prefill and bond price it set last, the Series the user picked. Tests: `tests/test_transaction_form_domain.py`; the UI tests per Transaction Type stay.
