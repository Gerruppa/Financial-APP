# `domain/transactions.py`

Transaction Types (Deposit, Withdrawal, Buy, Sell, Dividend, Cost, Currency Exchange, DRIP, Split, Cash Transfer, Security Transfer so far), `TransactionDraft` validation, `buy_or_sell(...)` (builds a Buy/Sell and derives its Actual Amount; a foreign-currency one carries the user's optional `fx_rate` and the `NbpRate`, from which the `tax_amount` property follows, or a `BrokerConversion` (the PLN the broker charged and the Account's FX Conversion Fee %): the trade stores the percent, and its `fx_conversion_fee` and `effective_fx_rate` follow, the fee counting as a cost in the Tax Amount; `cash_currency` says whether it is paid from PLN or from foreign cash of the Instrument's currency; the commission is in PLN, or in that foreign cash on an Account without PLN (`commission_currency`, #124, column added by migration 0034): taken from that cash, valued at the trade's rate in the Actual Amount, `commission_in_pln` and `costs_by_account`, at the NBP Rate in the Tax Amount; `commission_from_pln` is the part paid from PLN, which `lots.foreign_cash` leaves out of the foreign flow), `currency_exchange(...)` (PLN to a foreign Cash Currency or back, `to_pln`), `dividend(...)` (gross and withholding tax in the Instrument's or the chosen currency, the net amount into PLN at the user's rate or the NBP Rate, or the PLN the broker credited with a `BrokerConversion` (its fee a cost), or into foreign cash of that currency; no Instrument means interest), `cost(...)` (PLN, or from foreign cash valued at the NBP Rate), `deposit_or_withdrawal(...)` (PLN, or `quantity` of a foreign Cash Currency worth the Actual Amount at the user's `fx_rate` or the NBP Rate, the Tax Amount at the NBP Rate; `TransactionType.moves_cash_amount` marks the three, and `foreign_cash` holds such a Deposit as a Lot and closes Lots with such a Withdrawal like a Cost, #119), `drip(...)` (a Dividend reinvested into `quantity` units: a Lot costing the net amount, at the NBP Rate if foreign, no cash moving), `split(...)` (a `SplitRatio` X:Y, no amount), `cash_transfer(...)` (PLN to `target_account_id`, which `cash_balances` credits, or `quantity` of a foreign `cash_currency` worth the Actual Amount at the NBP Rate, #125: `moves_cash_amount` now covers it, and `lots.foreign_cash` moves the source's oldest cash Lots to the target with their dates and costs (`_move_out`, a shortfall at the NBP value leaving a negative Lot; `_receive`, by date, first closing a negative Lot like incoming cash)) and `security_transfer(...)` (units to `target_account_id`, no amount), `costs_by_account` (Costs, commissions and FX Conversion Fees) and `dividends_by_account`, `covering_exchange(buy)` (the "wymień walutę automatycznie" Exchange buying exactly the Buy's foreign amount), `cash_balances` (per Account, in any currency) and `lowest_cash_balance` (drives the insufficient-cash warning, per currency). `persistence/transactions.py` saves them (refusing PLN cash on an Account without PLN, #124, or a Cash Transfer to an Account without its currency, #125, from the dialog or an import; `add_transaction`, `add_transactions` (several at once, all or none), `update_transaction` and `delete_transaction`, each replaying the touched Positions to block a change that would leave a Sell or Security Transfer uncovered or a Split before the first Buy; `list_transactions`, newest first, for the Transakcje list; `transactions_in_entry_order`, by id, for the FIFO engine and everything replaying it: no caller reverses `list_transactions`).
