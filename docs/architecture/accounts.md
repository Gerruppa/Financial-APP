# `domain/accounts.py`

Account Types (Bonds and Other, #64, are stored as text, so new types need no migration), the Tax Regime registry (`tax_regime_for`; OKI is a placeholder, Other only describes the 6-month rule for selling things) and `AccountDraft`, which validates Account fields and raises `AccountError` with a Polish message. `persistence/accounts.py` saves Accounts (`list_accounts`, `add_account`, `update_account`) and enforces case-insensitive unique names. `delete_account` (L-28, #103) deletes an Account only while `account_holders` finds nothing naming it (`domain/accounts.py` `AccountHolders`: its Transactions, Transfers into it, its own Strategy, XTB account numbers mapped to it; `refusal` gives the Polish message) and forgets a View Scope on it; the Account dialog's `delete-account` (editing only, with `on_deleted`) shows the refusal or asks `confirm-delete-account`, and the shell's `account_deleted` switches a scope on it back to Total.
