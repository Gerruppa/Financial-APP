# `domain/lots.py`

The single FIFO engine (ADR-0003). `open_positions(transactions)` takes Transactions in entry order, sorts by date and the same-day order, and returns `Position`s (open `Lot`s with Actual `cost` and `tax_cost`, average price, value and result at a PLN price); a Split rescales open Lots (units × X/Y, costs kept) and a Security Transfer moves the oldest Lots to the target Account with their dates and costs; an uncovered Sell or Security Transfer raises `InsufficientQuantityError`, a Split before the Account held the Instrument `SplitBeforeFirstBuyError`. Same-day order: Split, Buy, Security Transfer, Sell, DRIP. `foreign_cash(transactions)` holds foreign cash as Lots per Account and currency (a foreign Cash Transfer moving them to its target, #125; `ForeignCash`, with the realised FX result; spending more than held only warns and leaves a negative Lot), and `fx_result` sums an Account's realised and unrealised FX result.
