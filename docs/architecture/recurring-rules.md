# Recurring Rules (#97, spec 3.3.1)

`domain/recurring.py` `RecurringRuleDraft` (a Deposit, Buy, Cost or Cash Transfer, `RECURRING_TYPES` (#147), of `amount` in one of the Account's Cash Currencies, a Buy naming its Instrument, a Cash Transfer its `target_account_id`, another Account; `Frequency` monthly, quarterly, semiannual, yearly from the month of `start_date`; `day_of_month` 1–31; optional `end_date`; `active`; `RecurringRuleError` with a Polish message) and `RecurringRule` (saved, with `id`). `occurrences(rule, until)`: the `scheduled_day` of each month due (the month's last day when shorter, a Saturday or Sunday moved to the next Monday, possibly into the next month) from `start_date` to `until` and `end_date`, compared on the moved day; `next_occurrence` (the settings list's "najbliższy"), `first_occurrence_after` (a new rule's default start, `new_rule_defaults`, and that of `repeating(transaction, today)`, "Powtarzaj…", counted from today or a Transaction dated ahead, never from an old one: a Buy's Actual Amount in PLN, any other's amount in its currency, a Cash Transfer's target carried over). `recurring_drafts(rules, transactions, skipped, today, account_id, bond_types)` gives the `RecurringDraft`s (rule, `due`) oldest first: an occurrence is covered by a Transaction linked to it (`TransactionDraft.recurring_rule_id` and `recurring_occurrence`, both or neither, kept by `update_transaction` like the Origin), else by an unlinked one on the rule's Account, of its type, for a Buy of its Instrument (of a Bond Series: of any Series of its bond type, `bond_types` by Instrument id, #149) and for a Cash Transfer to its target Account, within `MATCH_WINDOW` (±7 days): every rule's occurrences, oldest first, each take the nearest one still free (amounts never compared); skipped (rule, day) pairs and inactive rules give none; a Cash Transfer's Drafts show in the View of either Account. `RecurringDraft.link(draft)` names the occurrence, `transaction(nbp_rate)` is the Deposit, Cost or Cash Transfer "Zatwierdź" saves (named after the rule). `persistence/recurring_rules.py` (tables `recurring_rules`, `recurring_rule_skips`, columns `transactions.recurring_rule_id`/`recurring_occurrence`, migration 0033; `recurring_rules.target_account_id`, migration 0035): `list_rules`, `add_rule`, `update_rule` (checking the Account, its Cash Currency, the Instrument and a Cash Transfer's target holding the currency), `set_rule_active`, `delete_rule` (its Transactions unlinked, its skips gone), `delete_rules_of_account` (called by `delete_account`; also the Cash Transfers to the Account), `skip_occurrence`, `restore_occurrence`, `skipped_occurrences`, `skip_overdue` ("Pomiń zaległe": the Drafts before today), `pending_recurring_drafts` (the Bond Series' bond types from the Instruments) and `confirm_recurring_draft` (a foreign amount at the NBP Rate D-1); `delete_instrument` refuses an Instrument a rule buys; both tables are cleared by Wyczyść dane. `ui/recurring_rules.py`: `RecurringRulesSection` in Ustawienia below Limity wpłat (`recurring-rules`, rows `recurring-rule`, `add-recurring-rule`, `toggle-recurring-rule`, `edit-recurring-rule`, `delete-recurring-rule` with `confirm-delete-recurring-rule`) and `open_rule_dialog` (built on the page itself, so a list redraw or the closing transaction dialog leaves it; `rule-name`, `rule-account`, `rule-type`, `rule-target` (a Cash Transfer's, the other Accounts), `rule-instrument`, `rule-amount`, `rule-currency`, `rule-frequency`, `rule-day`, `rule-start`, `rule-end`, `rule-active`, `rule-error`, `save-rule`; a new rule's `rule-start` follows `rule-day` and `rule-frequency` to their next occurrence until typed; editing: `rule-skip` rows with `restore-skip`, and `skip-overdue`). `ui/recurring_drafts.py`: `recurring_drafts_list` above the Transakcje list, following the View Scope (`recurring-drafts`, rows `recurring-draft`, `confirm-recurring` for all but a Buy, `edit-recurring` opening the transaction dialog with `prefill_of(draft)` (`domain/transaction_form.py` `FormPrefill`, `initial_state(today, prefill)`; a Buy's quantity and price left empty) and `link=draft.link`, `skip-recurring`), and `recurring_drafts_count` in the header (`recurring-drafts-count`, "Cykliczne do zatwierdzenia: n" over every Account, opening Transakcje), apart from the bonds' list and count. The transaction dialog's `repeat-transaction` ("Powtarzaj…", an edited Deposit, Buy, Cost or Cash Transfer) opens the rule dialog with `repeating`. `instrument_labels` shows a Bond Series by its bond type in the rule list and the Drafts, `accounts_of` a Cash Transfer rule as "source → target". A rule change, an Account deleted (its rules go with it) and Wyczyść dane publish `Change.RECURRING_RULES`, which the Transakcje tab and the header count redraw on, with `Change.TRANSACTIONS`. Tests: `tests/test_recurring_domain.py`, `tests/test_recurring_repository.py`, `tests/test_recurring_ui.py`.
