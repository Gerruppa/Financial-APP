# Notifications: the header bell (#176)

`ui/notifications.py` `NotificationBell(engine, rates)` is the bell button `notifications-bell` in the header (between the eye and "Wesprzyj"), with a menu `notifications-menu` listing the reminders and a red badge `notifications-count` with the number of unread ones. `ui/shell.py` redraws `bell.content` on Transactions, prices, bond data, Term Deposits and Recurring Rules. The bell replaced the header's two "do zatwierdzenia" counters (the variants were prototyped on branch `prototype/header-bell`; C won: a number, not a dot), and the header's "Wyceny" button shortens to the day and month ("Wyceny: 09.10, 18:05").

A reminder (`domain/notifications.py` `Notification(key, text, path)`) is derived from the current state by `sources/notifications.py` `collect_notifications`, never stored; clicking one opens its tab. Kinds: bond and Term Deposit Drafts due (`/obligacje`), Recurring Rule Drafts due (`/transakcje`), Instruments the last refresh found no price for (Ustawienia), bonds (`bond_calendar` maturities) and open Term Deposits maturing within `MATURITY_DAYS` (30) (`/obligacje`) and Contribution Limits exceeded this year (Dashboard).

Read state: a key says the reminder's state (`drafts:<count>:<latest due>`, `prices:<refresh time>`, `maturing:<name>:<day>`, `limit:<type>:<year>:<over>`), so a changed reminder is new. Clicking the bell saves the listed keys as read in `app_settings` `ui.notifications_read` (`persistence/settings.py` `UI_NOTIFICATIONS_READ`, a JSON list, kept on clearing the data) and drops the number; the items stay bold (`notification-unread`, otherwise `notification`) until the next redraw. `domain/notifications.py` also has `unread`, `dump_read` and `parse_read`.
