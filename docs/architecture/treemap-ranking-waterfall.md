# Treemap, Account ranking, waterfall and after-tax toggle (#89, spec 6.1)

`domain/lots.py` Lots of a foreign Instrument carry an `FxBasis` (cost in its currency and in PLN at the market rate paid, `TransactionDraft.market_amount`, kept pro rata by `Lot.part`/`rest`), so `Position.fx_result(rate)` and `realized_fx_results` give the FX part of the result. `domain/lots.py` `replay` gives the Positions and both realised results from one replay; `domain/profit_sources.py` `profit_sources` (on `Dashboard.sources`, fed the scope's result and its FX part by `dashboard`) splits the tiles' result into price, FX, Dividends and interest (gross), costs and taxes (withholding), the price effect being the rest; `after_tax` adds the estimate. `Totals` gains `twr`, `estimated_tax` (`TaxRegime.unrealized_tax_rate`, 19% `BELKA_RATE` on Regular, at the Tax cost; Total offsets the Accounts) and `profit` (value − Net Deposits). `domain/account_ranking.py` (`ranking`, `sort_ranking`, `best_and_worst`, `treemap_tiles`) feeds `ui/account_ranking.py` (`accounts_treemap`, `chart-accounts-treemap`, a tile click switches the View Scope; `tile_colours(share, theme)` blends from the Theme's tile without P&L (`ui/theme.py` `TILE_PALE`, pale in Jasny and dark in Golden) to green or red, with `readable_label`'s black or white label; `AccountRanking`, `account-ranking`, rows `ranking-row`, headers `ranking-sort-<column>`, cells `ranking-best`/`ranking-worst`), both in the Total scope only; `ui/charts.py` draws `chart-waterfall` (an ECharts custom series) first among the charts; `DashboardPage` has the `after-tax` switch and the `total-estimated-tax` tile.
