# Dashboard charts (#73, spec 6.1)

`domain/charts.py` gives the data for the View Scope and period (`value_series`, `profit_series` value − Net Deposits as the sheet, `xirr_series` from the "from" day through `metrics.xirr_to_each` at most `XIRR_POINTS` days, `class_shares` on the last day of each `Grouping` from `DayValue.account_classes`, `dividends_by_year` per Account); `domain/dashboard.py` adds today's allocation (`Dashboard.classes`, `ClassShare`). `ui/charts.py` `dashboard_charts` draws the 9 charts (each through `ui/theme.py` `echart`, in the window's Theme) (markers `chart-<name>`, the History ones after `chart-history-pending`; class shares switched by `grouping-<key>`) below the Dashboard's tables; JS formatters (`:valueFormatter`) show Polish numbers. Chart zoom (#154): `zoom_button` puts a button (`zoom-<name>`) in the bottom right corner of every chart card (all of `_card`'s, the class shares and the Accounts treemap), opening a copy of the chart's options in a dialog of 95vw × 90vh (`chart-<name>-zoomed`, closed by `zoom-close` or Esc and then deleted); a `timeline` chart (value, profit, XIRR, drawdown, class shares, `pln_lines_chart`) gets `dataZoom` (slider and mouse wheel) there only; a point click on the zoomed treemap closes it and switches the View Scope. ECharts resizes itself with the dialog (NiceGUI's `ResizeObserver`).
