# `persistence/reset.py`

`clear_portfolio(engine)` backs the database up into the `backups` folder next to it (no backup, nothing cleared: `BackupError`), then removes the rows of every model declaring `OnClear.DELETE` (Accounts, Instruments, Transactions and what hangs off them), children first by `Base.metadata.sorted_tables`, plus the remembered XTB matches and `BENCHMARKS_SEEDED` in `app_settings`, and restores `domain/instruments.py` `DEFAULT_ASSET_CLASSES` (the `OnClear.KEEP` models stay: NBP Rate caches, bond data, Contribution Limits, `app_settings`). A new model must declare `on_clear`, or `tests/test_clear_data_repository.py` fails; `portfolio_counts` feeds the warning. `ui/reset.py` is its card at the bottom of Ustawienia: a warning with the counts, then the typed phrase `USUŃ WSZYSTKO` enables the final button.
