# Term Deposits (#160, spec 3.3.2)

`domain/term_deposits.py` `TermDepositDraft` (Account, name, `principal`, `start_date`, `term_count` of `TermUnit` months or days, `annual_rate` percent, `Capitalization` at maturity, monthly, quarterly, semiannual, yearly; `renew`; `closed_on`; `maturity`; `TermDepositError`, Polish) and `TermDeposit` (saved, `id`). `interest_periods(deposit, tax_rate)` gives the `InterestPeriod`s (capital × rate × actual days / `DAYS_A_YEAR` 365, gross and Belka each rounded to the grosz half up, a capitalised period's net added to the next capital; periods ending every `Capitalization.months` from the start by `bonds.add_months`, the last on maturity; a broken deposit's periods only up to `closed_on`). `paid_periods(deposits, transactions)` gives the day each period (`PeriodKey`: deposit id, period end) was paid: interest linked to it (`TransactionDraft.term_deposit_id`/`term_deposit_period`, both or neither, only on interest without an Instrument; kept by `update_transaction`), else free interest (no Instrument, Lot or link) on the Account within `MATCH_WINDOW` ±7 days, nearest first. `accrued_interest`/`Accruals(deposits, tax_rates_of(accounts), transactions).on(day)` give the `AccruedInterest` (gross, tax, `net`) by Account: the periods ended and not paid by the day plus the running period's days so far. `deposit_drafts(deposits, accounts, transactions, day, account_id)` gives the `InterestDraft`s (`transaction()`: the linked interest) and `DepositMaturityDraft`s (`final_capital`, `renewal(rate)`) by `due`, interest before maturity; `deposit_rows` the list's `DepositRow`s (accrued, `break_value`, `days_left`); `locked_capital(deposits, day)` the principal of the deposits running by Account. `persistence/term_deposits.py` (table `term_deposits`, columns `transactions.term_deposit_id`/`term_deposit_period`, migration 0036, `OnClear.DELETE`): `list_deposits`, `add_deposit`/`update_deposit` (a Deposit Account holding PLN), `close_deposit` (ended or broken), `renew_deposit` (both or neither), `delete_deposit` (its interest unlinked), `delete_deposits_of_account` (called by `delete_account`). `persistence/deposit_drafts.py` `pending_deposit_drafts`, `confirm_interest`. Value: `domain/account_value.py` `AccountValue.accrued` (in `value`, `holds`), `sources/todays_view.py` `TodaysView.deposits`/`accrued`, `domain/dashboard.py` `dashboard(..., accrued=)` (`Totals.accrued` in `value`, counted as `CASH_CLASS`, and in `profit_sources(..., accrued)` as gross interest less its tax), `domain/portfolio_history.py` `daily_history(..., accruals=)` (`HistoryInputs.accruals`) adding the net to each Account and `CASH_CLASS` day by day. `domain/save_warnings.py` `save_warnings(..., deposits=)` gives `LockedCash` when a save takes an Account's PLN cash on a day it touched below `locked_capital`, not below before (`sources/transaction_saving.py` passes `list_deposits`). Passive income: `IncomeKind.DEPOSIT_INTEREST` ("Odsetki z lokat") for linked interest, `forecast_deposit_interest` for the `InterestDraft`s up to 12 months ahead. UI: `ui/term_deposits.py` `DepositsSection` in the Dochód stały tab (`term-deposits`, `add-deposit` (only with a Deposit Account holding PLN), rows `deposit-row` opening the dialog, `deposits-empty`) and `open_deposit_dialog` (`deposit-dialog`: `deposit-account`, `deposit-name`, `deposit-principal`, `deposit-start`, `deposit-term`, `deposit-term-unit`, `deposit-rate`, `deposit-capitalization`, `deposit-renew`, a new one's `deposit-capital` (none, a Deposit or a Cash Transfer from another Account, saved through `checked_save`; the Deposit by default while `needs_capital` says the Account's PLN cash at the end of the start day is below the principal, else none, following the fields until the user picks one) with `deposit-capital-hint`, `deposit-summary` (maturity and net interest at the end), `deposit-error`, `save-deposit`; editing: `deposit-break-date` with `break-deposit`, `delete-deposit` with `confirm-delete-deposit`); `ui/deposit_drafts.py` `interest_row` (`deposit-draft`, `confirm-deposit-interest`, `edit-deposit-interest` opening `interest-dialog` with `interest-date`, `interest-gross`, `interest-tax`, `save-interest`) and `maturity_row` (`end-deposit`, `renew-deposit` opening `renew-dialog` with `renew-rate`, `save-renew`), drawn by `ui/bond_drafts.py` `drafts_list` among the bonds' Drafts by day and counted in `drafts_count`; the Portfolio card shows `account-accrued` ("Narosłe odsetki lokat"). A deposit change publishes `Change.TERM_DEPOSITS` (in `TODAYS_VALUE`; History and the header count subscribe to it). Tests: `tests/test_term_deposits_domain.py`, `tests/test_term_deposits_repository.py`, `tests/test_term_deposits_valuation.py`, `tests/test_term_deposits_ui.py`.
