# Saying of the day (#178)

`domain/sayings.py` `Saying` (`text`, `work`), the fixed list `SAYINGS` (lines from `AUTHOR` Bartosz Walaszek's cartoons found in published collections of their quotes, for now Kapitan Bomba) and `saying_of_the_day(day, menu)`: the list cycles by the day's ordinal, so a saying stays all day and a redraw does not swap it; `menu` takes the next one, so the two never repeat each other. Named "saying", not "quote", because a Quote is an Instrument's price. `ui/sayings.py` `saying_card(day)` is the Dashboard's card (`dashboard-saying`), a third of the row beside the Investment Goal (the goal's card stretched to its height) or the whole row alone; `menu_saying(day)` is the line at the foot of the shell's menu (`menu-saying`). Both are hidden by one element `sayings` ("Cytat dnia (Dashboard i menu)") in Wygląd i widoczność; Podstawowy shows it, so a Podstawowy saved before it still reads as Podstawowy.
