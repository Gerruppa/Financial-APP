# EDO valuation (L-3, spec 3.4.1)

`domain/bond_valuation.py` `bond_value(series, purchase_day, day, cpi)` values one bond by the MF issue letters (the yearly rates compounded unrounded, linear accrual within the year over its actual days, the bond rounded once to the grosz, half up; 29 February anniversaries fall on 28 February; held at maturity value after it) and gives a `BondValue` (value, period from 1, its `PeriodRate`, the most uncertain `RateLabel` of the rates used). A rate neither MF nor the user gave is `Cpi.rate`: GUS CPI year on year of the anniversary month − `CPI_LAG_MONTHS` (2), − 100 floored at 0 (deflation leaves the margin, as MF paid), + margin, "szacowana (CPI)", or from the last reading "prognoza (ostatnie CPI)". Every type is valued: ROS and ROD as EDO, TOS compounding its one fixed rate yearly, the `SHORT_TYPES` OTS earning its rate over its days out of `DAYS_A_YEAR` 365 (L-7), and the `domain/bonds.py` `COUPON_TYPES` COI, ROR, DOR (L-8) at the nominal plus the current period's coupon accrued (`coupons(...)` / `BondPrice.coupons` give the `Coupon`s paid, 100 × rate × the period's share of a year, rounded); a ROR or DOR month MF has not published takes `ReferenceRates.rate` (the NBP reference rate in force `NBP_LAG_BUSINESS_DAYS` 10 weekdays before the first of the period's calendar month, + margin, "szacowana (NBP)", "prognoza (ostatnia stopa NBP)" when fixed after `BondData.reference_known_until`, the day the copy was fetched; `BondData.reference_rates`, loaded by `load_bond_data`; the bond data source keys `MF`, `GUS`, `NBP_REFERENCE` live in `persistence/bond_data.py`). `rate_text` gives "<rate> · rok <n>" ("miesiąc <n>" for ROR and DOR, no number for an OTS) for the Portfolio and the Dochód stały tab. An OTS redeemed early forfeits its interest (`domain/bond_redemption.py` `FORFEITING_TYPES`), counted as its fee, so its Net Value is the nominal. `BondData(series by Instrument id, CPI readings).price(id, day)` gives a `BondPrice` (`None` for other types or without a first rate), whose `of(lot)` values a Lot from its purchase day (at its cost without a value; a Lot dated after the day at its nominal); `persistence/bond_series.py` `load_bond_data` loads it. `most_uncertain` picks the label shown on a Position row from all its Lots. `domain/lots.py` `LotPrice` protocol and `UnitPrice = Decimal | LotPrice`: `Position.value`/`result` take either, so the Dashboard, Multi-asset and History value bonds per Lot. `domain/valuation.py` `instrument_price(..., bond=)` gives `PriceStatus.BOND` (no badge; a Manual Price still overrides, badge "ręczna"; a Series without its first rate `Badge.NO_BOND_RATE` "brak oprocentowania"; a treasury bond that is no Series, e.g. DS or IZ on Catalyst, is quoted like any Instrument through Bossa, the class's default order since migration 0025); `sources/current_prices.py` builds it from the `BondData` that `sources/todays_view.py` loads (`load_bond_data`), for `TodaysRates.today()`. `daily_history(..., bonds=)` and `HistoryInputs.bonds` value Bond Series day by day. The Portfolio shows a Series' value per bond with "<rate> · rok <n>" and the label (`bond-rate-label`), and its Lots each Lot's rate and value. `tests/test_bond_parity.py` compares each EDO row of the sheet's Obligacje tab per bond within 0.01 PLN (the sheet's valuation day inferred from its first-year rows); the Historia parity test loads the MF file from `tests/fixtures/mf/` and has no bond deviation.
