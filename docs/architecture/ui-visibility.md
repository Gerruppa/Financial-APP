# Wygląd i widoczność (#155)

`domain/ui_visibility.py` lists the `ELEMENTS` that can be hidden (`Element` key, Polish label, group: the Dashboard tiles `tile-<key>`, sections `since-snapshot`, `investment-goal`, `sayings` (the saying of the day on the Dashboard and in the menu, #178), `account-table`, `contribution-limits`, `account-ranking` (treemap and ranking), `currency-exposure`, `strategy`, the charts `chart-<name>` and the tabs `tab-<path>`, `tab_key`; Dashboard, Portfolio, Transakcje, Ustawienia and Pomoc have none), the View Profiles (`Profile` BASIC, FULL, CUSTOM; `preset_hidden`, `profile_of`) and the text they are kept as (`visibility_setting`/`parse_visibility`, a JSON list in `app_settings` key `ui.hidden`, `persistence/settings.py` `UI_HIDDEN`, one for every View Scope, kept by clearing the data; nothing saved is Pełny). `ui/visibility.py` `Layout` is the window's hidden set (`shows(key)`, `hide(hidden)` saves it and calls its `on_change` listeners, through which the shell publishes `Change.LAYOUT`); `VisibilitySection` is its card in Ustawienia, with the Theme's toggle at its top (`ui-theme`, `ui/theme.py` `theme_section`, #174) (`ui-visibility`, `ui-profile`, presets `ui-profile-basic`/`ui-profile-full`, `ui-visibility-restore` back to Pełny, a switch `ui-visibility-<key>` per element). `DashboardPage` (redrawn on `LAYOUT`) leaves out what is hidden, still computing the strategy for the allocation chart's targets; `dashboard_charts(..., shows)` skips the hidden charts and `chart-history-pending` when every History chart is hidden; the shell's menu (redrawn on `LAYOUT`) leaves out a hidden tab, whose route still opens it.
