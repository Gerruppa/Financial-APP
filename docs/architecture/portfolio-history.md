# `domain/portfolio_history.py` (#68, spec 6.1, ADR-0006)

`daily_history(transactions, instruments, prices, rates, first, last)` gives a `DayValue` per day (value per Account and Asset Class, PLN cash in `CASH_CLASS`, foreign cash in `FOREIGN_CASH_CLASS`; `net_deposits` from Deposits and Withdrawals, `account_net_deposits` also from Cash and Security Transfers, the latter at that day's value, else the moved Lots' cost, replayed up to the transfer in the same-day order so a Sell of the moved units on the target Account that day stays covered; `unpriced` Instruments valued at cost). Positions come from `open_positions` replayed up to each day; prices and NBP Rates (published on day D, not D-1) carry forward; Bond Series stay at cost. A `HistoryPrice` with `adjusted_on` (from a history source) is in that day's units, so it is rescaled by the Splits recorded in between. `YahooSource.history` gives Yahoo's daily `close` (never `adjclose`), saved as `YAHOO_HISTORY`; `QuoteHistory` tries `HISTORY_SOURCES` (Yahoo, analizy.pl, then Stooq; one fixed order for now, not per Asset Class) and `latest_quotes` ignores `HISTORY_ONLY_SOURCES`; only the `SPLIT_ADJUSTED_SOURCES` (Stooq, Yahoo history) carry `adjusted_on`. `sources/portfolio_history.py` `PortfolioHistory`: `fetch(progress)` fills the cache from each Instrument's and currency's first Transaction to yesterday (failures returned, not raised), `values()` computes from the cache alone through `run.cpu_bound` (injectable) and keeps it until `invalidate()`. `values()` ignores a computation overtaken by an `invalidate()`. The shell wires it through `ui/history.py` (#71): `SharedHistory` per database (shared by its windows) fetches in `run.io_bound` after the start-up price refresh (only with `refresh_at_start`) with the header status "Historia: pobieranie… (n/N)" (on one line, so the header keeps its height), then red "Historia: n błędów" listing the failures (`history-status`); a Transaction save or price refresh invalidates it and recomputes in the background; `build_shell(..., history=...)` takes a test instance (tests pass an inline `compute`). `prices_on` and `rates_on` give one day's PLN prices and NBP Rates by the same pricing (the Portfolio XLSX report of a past year, #241); `sources/portfolio_history.py` `history_inputs` reads the cache for them and for `PortfolioHistory.inputs()`.
