# Contribution Limits (#95, spec 8.1)

`domain/contribution_limits.py` (`LIMITED_TYPES` IKE, IKZE, OIPE; `contribution_type` (a Deposit into such an Account, a Cash Transfer into one from an Account of another type), `contributions` per type in a year, `LimitBar` (`left`, `over`, `share`), `limit_bars` for the View Scope (Total: each type with an active Account or a contribution that year; an Account: its type's bar over all its type's Accounts), `over_limit(before, after, ...)` (only a save adding to a year over its limit) and `over_limit_warning`, `parse_limit` (to the grosz), `IKZE_SELF_EMPLOYED` hint; `ContributionLimitError`). `persistence/contribution_limits.py` (`load_limits`, `add_year`, `set_limits` (a year's types at once), `delete_year` refusing a year with a limit; table `contribution_limits`, migration 0029 seeding 2026; `clear_portfolio` keeps them). `ui/contribution_limits.py`: `ContributionLimitsSection` in Ustawienia below Konta, redrawn after an Account is saved (`AccountsSection.on_changed`), an import or a data clear (columns `limit-column-<year>-<type>` sharing the row, fields `limit-<year>-<type>`, `limit-paid-<year>-<type>`, the IKZE self-employed limit as its own label `limit-self-employed-<year>` rather than a field hint (#117), `limits-save-<year>` "Ustaw limity", `limits-delete-<year>`, `limits-new-year` with `limits-add-year`), `limits_section` on the Dashboard below the Account table (`contribution-limits`, `limit-bar-<type>`); the warning after a save that takes a year over its limit comes from `domain/save_warnings.py` (#110), whichever place saved.

Before a save (#273): `TransactionForm.limit_bar` (a Deposit into a limited Account, its type's bar of the form's year without the edited Transaction; `FormContext.limits` from `load_limits`), `limit_hint` ("Do limitu IKE 2026 zostało: …", and by how much the typed amount goes over; "Brak limitu wpłat …" in a year without one) and `limit_split` (a new PLN Deposit only, never an edit or a Recurring Rule occurrence). `domain/contribution_limits.py` `split_over_limit` gives a `LimitSplit`: `within` up to the limit (`None` once it is used up) and `remainder` on another Account, commented "nadwyżka ponad limit …"; `remainder_accounts` are active PLN Accounts without a limit and not PPK, the one deposited into last first (the default). `sources/transaction_saving.py` `save_split` saves both or none. In the dialog (`ui/transaction_dialog.py`): `transaction-limit-hint`, `transaction-limit-help` ("Jak działają limity?", opening `ui/limits_guide.py` `open_limits_guide`, card `limits-guide`: the year's limits, what counts, what to do with an over-deposit), and while a split is possible `transaction-limit-remainder` with `transaction-limit-split` ("Podziel wpłatę"); "Zapisz" still saves the Deposit as typed, with the warning. Imports and Recurring Rule Drafts only warn.
