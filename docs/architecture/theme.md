# Theme: Jasny and Golden (#174)

`domain/themes.py` `Theme` (`LIGHT` "jasny", `GOLDEN` "golden", `label` Jasny/Golden) and `parse_theme` (nothing or unknown text is Jasny); it is kept in `app_settings` key `ui.theme` (`persistence/settings.py` `UI_THEME`, one for every View Scope, kept by clearing the data; no migration). `ui/theme.py` `ThemeChoice(engine)` is the window's Theme (`choose(theme)` saves it and calls its `on_change` listeners; the shell's listener reloads the window, so every tab and chart is built again in it). `apply_theme(theme)` styles the page being built: `CSS` (colour variables `--k-*` on `body.theme-jasny`/`body.theme-golden`, the `k-` classes `k-header`, `k-side`, `k-menu-item`/`k-active`, `k-muted`, `k-hero`, `k-accent`, `k-highlight`, `k-icon`, and `k-band-<status>` from `BAND_COLOURS`, the Investment Strategy's band rows, pale in Jasny and deep in Golden, readable against `TEXT_COLOURS`; `THEME_COLOURS` gives each Theme's `page`, `card`, `hover`, `field` (a highlighted field, `k-field` on a filled select: the Account in the transaction, Recurring Rule and Term Deposit dialogs) and text tones (`text`, `muted`, `gain`, `loss`, `warning`), from which the CSS's `--k-<name>` variables are built, and a page names `k-muted`, `k-gain`, `k-loss`, `k-warning` and `k-hover` (a row's hover) instead of Tailwind's `text-gray-500`, `text-green-700`, `hover:bg-gray-100` and the like, which do not read in Golden; the Accounts treemap's `TILE_PALE` (per Theme), `TILE_GAIN`, `TILE_LOSS` and `TILE_UNKNOWN`, with `readable_label(background)` (black or white by WCAG `luminance`); `tests/test_theme_contrast.py` checks WCAG AA 4.5:1 for the bands, the tiles and every text tone on the page, a card and a hovered row; every `q-card` gets the border and radius, and `q-tab-panels` a transparent background (Quasar's white showed as a band beside Ustawienia's narrower cards); Golden draws gold mountains, an inline SVG, behind the header and at the foot of the menu), Quasar's colours (`primary`; custom `ink` for the header's text, `side`/`side-active` for the menu, `attention` for pending Drafts, `negative`) and dark mode for Golden. A page names these tokens, never a colour (`color=primary`, not `indigo`). ECharts draws on canvas, so every chart goes through `ui/theme.py` `echart(options)`, which passes `chart_theme()`: none for Jasny, a dark theme with gold first for Golden; `current_theme()` reads the Theme the page put in its client's storage (Jasny outside a page). `LINE_COLOURS` colour the Dashboard's main chart and the sparklines, `LABEL_COLOURS` the waterfall's change labels. `theme_section(choice)` is the toggle in Wygląd i widoczność (`ui-theme`); the header's `theme-toggle` switches to the other Theme. The design was chosen with a prototype on branch `prototype/dashboard-redesign`.
