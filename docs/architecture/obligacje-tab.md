# Obligacje tab and Net Value (L-4, spec 3.4.1)

`domain/bond_redemption.py` `EARLY_REDEMPTION_FEES` (per bond type, from the first month of sale each fee applies to; `early_redemption_fee(series)` dates a Series by `domain/bonds.py` `sale_month`) and `net_value(price, lot, tax_rate)` give a `NetValue` of one bond (gross, the fee capped at the accrued interest, none from `maturity`, and the tax rounded half up on the profit over the Lot's Tax cost; `net`). `TaxRegime.withheld_tax_rate` is the Belka withheld on bonds (none on IKE, IKZE, OIPE, PPK). `domain/bonds.py` `add_months`, `maturity`; `domain/bond_valuation.py` `next_anniversary`. `domain/bond_holdings.py` `bond_holdings(positions, instruments, accounts, bonds, day, account_id)` gives a `SeriesHolding` per Series and Account (largest first) with its `LotHolding`s; `ui/bonds.py` `BondsPage` draws it at `/obligacje`, the Dochód stały tab (#160: the Term Deposits' section above the bonds; `bonds`, rows `bond-row` expanding to `bond-lots`, `bond-net`, `bond-totals`, `bonds-empty`, an `ui/empty_state.py` `empty_state` whose button opens the Transaction dialog), redrawn like the Portfolio. `domain/dashboard.py` adds each valued Bond Series' fee and withheld tax (`_bond_deductions`) to `estimated_tax`, outside the PIT-38 offset; the tile reads "Szacowany podatek i opłaty".
