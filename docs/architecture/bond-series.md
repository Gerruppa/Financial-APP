# Bond Series (L-2, spec 3.4.1)

An Instrument named like `EDO1036` (`domain/bonds.py` `SERIES_NAME`, `bond_type_of`) in the treasury bond class (`TREASURY_BOND_CLASS`, the one in `prices.STAGE_3_CLASSES`) is a Bond Series (`Instrument.is_bond_series`). `series_name(type, purchase_day)` names the Series a purchase buys (type + month and year of maturity, `TERM_MONTHS`). `BondSeries` merges the saved MF terms (`mf`, `None` while the file lacks it) with the user's `RateCorrection` (margin and per-period rates, `None` where MF's stands, so later MF rates show through; `correction_of` keeps only what differs): `rates` (one per `PERIODS`), `first_rate`, `margin`, `rollover_price` (MF's, else `DEFAULT_ROLLOVER_PRICE` 99.90, `None` for the `FAMILY_TYPES` ROS/ROD). `persistence/bond_series.py`: `list_bond_series`, `get_bond_series`, `save_rate_correction` (table `bond_rate_corrections`, migration 0023, which also clears the Manual Prices the Stage 1 import set on Bond Series) and `series_instrument(name)` (finds or creates the Series' Instrument in PLN with the MF ISIN). The "+" dialog's bond mode (`transaction-bond-mode`, on by default for a new Buy on a Bonds Account; `transaction-bond-type`, `transaction-bond-series`, `transaction-bond-help` opening the bond help window (see bond-guide), `transaction-rollover`) hides the Instrument and commission, prices one bond at 100 zł or the Rollover price and creates the Series on save (removed again if the save fails); an edited Buy of a Bond Series opens in it. On a Regular Account the bond mode shows `transaction-bond-hint` (`TransactionForm._bond_hint`, `FormLayout.bond_hint`) to use a Bonds Account; the Buy is refused at the save with the same advice (`account_rules.refusal`, see account-rules and transaction-form). `ui/bond_series.py` shows each Series' rates in Ustawienia > Instrumenty (`bond-rates`) and corrects them (`edit-bond-rates`; fields `bond-margin`, `bond-rate-<n>`, `save-bond-rates`, `restore-mf-rates`). The sheet import no longer sets a Manual Price on bonds. An older purchase picks its Series (L-26, #102) in `transaction-bond-series-choice` (`domain/bonds.py` `series_of_type`, the MF Series of the type newest first, labelled "<Series> · sprzedaż MM.RRRR"; the date's own by default, a pick kept until the type changes); `sold_on` (the sale window, a Rollover up to `ROLLOVER_GRACE` 7 days after it) adds "data zakupu poza sprzedażą serii (…)" to `transaction-bond-series`.
